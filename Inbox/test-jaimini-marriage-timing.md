Testing Jaimini Chara Dasha for Timing Marriage

After studying multiple charts where Jaimini Chara Dasha was applied for marriage timing, a clear pattern emerges. The marriage pada (Arudha of 7th house) and the placement of Darakaraka are critical.

In the example chart with Cancer ascendant, the 7th house is Capricorn. Mars rules Capricorn and is placed in Aquarius (11th from Capricorn). Counting 11 from Aquarius gives Sagittarius as the marriage pada. The native married during Sagittarius Chara Dasha.

Key principles observed:
1. The sign containing the marriage pada must be activated by Chara Dasha
2. The darakaraka planet's placement strengthens the timing
3. Jupiter's aspect on the pada sign often brings the event forward
4. Rahu/Ketu conjunction with pada delays or complicates the event

This suggests that for marriage timing using Jaimini, focusing solely on Vimshottari may miss the sign-based activation that Chara Dasha captures. The sign-based system accounts for the environment and partner characteristics better than planet-based dashas.
