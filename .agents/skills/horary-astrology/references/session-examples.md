## Example 4: TikTok Live — Moon VOC FAILED (Counterexample)

**Question:** "Will my older brother go live on TikTok around 1-2 AM?"
**Cast:** 2026-07-12 23:51 ICT @ 19.91°N, 99.83°E (Chiang Rai)
**ASC:** ♈ 16.9°

### Chart Details

| Element | Found | Meaning |
|---------|-------|---------|
| Querent | ♂ Mars (ASC ruler, Aries) | BooM |
| Brother | ☿ Mercury (3rd ruler, Gemini) | Sibling |
| Moon position | ♊ 26.27° → **in 3rd house (siblings)** | Emotionally in the right place |
| Moon VOC? | **YES** — last aspect separating ♀ Venus, no applying in ♊ | Should mean "nothing develops" |
| Mars vs Mercury aspect | 41° — NO major aspect | No direct connection |
| Mercury's house | ♋ **4th house** (home) | Brother is at home → DOING a live from home |
| Moon future aspect | Will ☌ ☿ Mercury in ♋ in ~24h | Delayed, but coming |

### Result: BROTHER WENT LIVE despite Moon VOC ❌

| Why the prediction failed | Explanation |
|---------------------------|-------------|
| **Moon in 3rd house** | Moon was physically in the siblings house — emotional connection to the matter outweighed VOC |
| **Moon about to ☌ Mercury** | VOC in current sign, but Moon will ☌ Mercury (the significator) in ~24h in next sign — energy was building, not absent |
| **Dispositorship** | Moon ♋ rules ♋ — Mercury was in ♋, meaning Moon "owns" Mercury's position. Indirect connection through rulership |
| **4th house misinterpretation** | Mercury in 4th = "brother at home" — but for content creators, this means **broadcasting from home**, not being idle |

### Key Takeaways for Moon VOC

1. **Moon VOC is NOT absolute** — especially when Moon is in the house of the question (3rd for siblings, 7th for partners, etc.)
2. **Check Moon's next sign** — if it will aspect the significator within 30° of entering the next sign, the matter may develop soon
3. **Check dispositorship** — if the planet Moon rules (Moon's sign = Cancer) contains the significator, there's an indirect link
4. **Interpret houses by ACTIVITY, not location** — Mercury in 4th = working/creating from home, not "staying home doing nothing"

## Radicality Rules (Revised)

A chart must pass these checks before interpretation — but **Moon VOC is not absolute**:

| Rule | Guideline | Exception / Nuance |
|------|-----------|-------------------|
| ASC 3°–27° | Early (<3°) = matter too new; Late (>27°) = past its peak | Still interpret with caveat |
| Moon not VOC | VOC = no major aspects before leaving sign | **If Moon will enter the quesited's sign within 30° and aspect the significator there, the matter may still develop after a delay** |
| Saturn not in 1st/7th | Saturn in 1st = querent blocked; in 7th = question denied | Saturn in 12th is neutral — hidden obstacles, not querent block |

## ⚠️ Flatlib Personal Orbs — Not Fixed Orbs (Added 27 Jul 2026)

This session revealed a critical error in earlier VOC calculations. The script and skill previously used **fixed aspect orbs** (sq=7°, opp=8°, etc.) for Moon VOC detection. This is **WRONG**.

flatlib (the library BooM's system is based on) uses **PERSONAL ORBS per planet**:

```
Sun=15°, Moon=12°, Mercury=7°, Venus=7°, Mars=8°, Jupiter=9°, Saturn=9°
```

**Logic:** `if obj1.orb() < deviation AND obj2.orb() < deviation: skip` — the aspect is valid if at least ONE planet's personal orb >= the current angular deviation from exact.

**Practical impact on Example 4 (TikTok Live):**

The Moon VOC call in that session might need rechecking — Moon ♊ 26.27° — did it have any cross-sign applying aspect with Traditional 7 planets that was within personal orbs but OUTSIDE fixed aspect orbs?

**Rule for future sessions:** Always use flatlib personal orbs for VOC determination, never fixed aspect orbs. See `references/moon-voc-calculation.md` for the complete reference.
