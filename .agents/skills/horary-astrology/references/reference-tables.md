## Sign Classification (Tajik/Prashna)

Determines whether the situation will change:

| Type | Signs | Meaning in Horary |
|------|-------|-------------------|
| **Chara** (Movable/Cardinal) | ♈♋♎♑ | **Situation will change** — the matter is active/developing |
| **Sthira** (Fixed) | ♉♌♏♒ | **Situation stays as-is** — no change, matter is stuck |
| **Dwisvabhava** (Dual/Mutable) | ♊♍♐♓ | **Changes with difficulty** — matter shifts but slowly |

**Rule:** The rising sign type tells you the *nature* of the answer regardless of aspects:
- Chara ASC → outcome will change current conditions
- Sthira ASC → things remain as they are
- Dual ASC → mixed, requires effort

Additionally:
- **Sirshodaya** signs (Aries, Taurus, Gemini, Cancer, Leo, Libra, Scorpio, Sagittarius) — rise with head first → direct, manifesting results quickly
- **Prushtodaya** signs (Virgo, Capricorn, Aquarius, Pisces) — rise with back first → delayed, hidden, difficult results

## Checking the Querent's Sincerity

Before reading, assess whether the querent is sincere (from `prashna-tantra.pdf`):

### Signs the querent is NOT honest (insincere):
- Moon in ASC + Saturn in a quadrant + Mercury combust
- Mars + Mercury aspect Moon in ASC
- Malefic joins the ASC
- Jupiter or Mercury cast an inimical glance on Lord of 7th

### Signs the querent IS sincere:
- A benefic planet joins the ASC
- Navamsa Lagna is strong and aspected by benefics

**Application:** If the querent appears insincere, weigh the reading cautiously — the answer may be unreliable because the question itself is not genuine.
