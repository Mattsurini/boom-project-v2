### Communication timing note — partner calls/messages

For questions like **"when will my partner call?"**:

1. Treat partner as **H7/L7**.
2. Treat the contact channel as **Mercury / H3** (call, message, communication).
3. The Moon is the clock: scan the Moon's next applying aspects before sign change.
4. If Moon's next exact aspect is to Mercury or the H3 ruler, give that time window as the first contact window.
5. If Moon separates from L7 but applies to Mercury/Venus, read as: partner energy exists, but contact comes via message/call/social rather than immediate smooth union.
6. Opposition/square to Mercury = contact can happen but awkward, delayed, short, or emotionally unsatisfying; trine/sextile = smoother.
7. Always state whether the aspect is applying or separating and convert degree distance using Moon speed (~12–13°/day) into local time.

### Expanded Prashna Tantra Questions (by House)

From `prashna-tantra.pdf` (Neelakanta), detailed interpretations for house-based questions:

**1st House (Self, health, general well-being)**
- Lord of ASC in ASC or with benefics → querent free from worry
- Lagna + Moon both weak → object of query fails
- Navamsa Lagna aspected by benefics → favorable results
- Past/Present/Future: read from planets in Musaripha (past), conjunction (present), Ithasala (future) with ASC lord

**2nd House (Money, wealth, possessions)**
- Lord of 2nd in Ithasala with Lord of 1st or Moon → ✅ gain of money
- Moon + Lord of ASC + Lord of 2nd in conjunction or mutual aspect in 2nd/Kendra/Trine → immediate gain
- Moon in 4th or 7th, Sun in 10th, benefic in ASC → immediate money
- Jupiter in ASC → gain

**3rd House (Siblings, communication, short trips)**
- Lord of 3rd aspects 3rd, or 3rd house + lord aspected by benefics → siblings healthy & happy
- Lord of 3rd in 6th with Ithasala to Lord of 6th → brothers in bad health
- Lord of 3rd combust → dangerous disease for sibling

**4th House (Home, land, property, father)**
- For farming/land: Lagna = querent, 7th = lessee, 10th = production, 4th = final profit
- Malefics in 7th → lessee suffers
- Malefics in 10th → no benefit even with good crop
- Malefics in 4th → final result unfavorable

**5th House (Children, romance, creativity, pregnancy)**
- Lagna or Moon in Muthasila with Lord of 5th → will have children
- Lord of 5th in Lagna + Lord of Lagna + Moon in 5th → early childbirth
- Dual sign ASC + benefics → twins
- Lagna + Lord of 5th in masculine signs → male child
- For **pregnancy timing**: number of Navamsas passed in Lagna = months pregnant; remaining Navamsas = months until delivery
- **Child survival**: Lord of 12th in Kendra with benefics → child lives; Lord of 12th malefic/combust → child dies

**6th House (Work, health, service, enemies)**
- For **disease**: Lord of Lagna + Moon in Ithasala with benefics → disease cured
- Lord of 8th in ASC → patient dies
- Retrograde planets in 1st/4th/7th/10th in Ithasala with Moon → patient dies
- ASC in Dwadasamsa of Sun → patient dies
- For **servants/cattle**: Lord of ASC + Moon in 6th, or Lord of 6th in ASC → ✅ will get

**7th House (Marriage, partnership, contracts, open enemies)**
- Lord of 7th in Ithasala with Lord of ASC or Moon → spouse secured without seeking
- Lord of ASC or Moon in 7th → spouse secured with effort
- Lord of ASC in Musaripha with Moon or Ithasala with Lord of 7th → spouse without effort
- If the Ithasala planet is combust/afflicted → will NOT succeed
- **Bride's character check**: Fixed sign ASC + fixed Lord + fixed Moon → pure; Movable → not pure
- Moon + Mars in movable/dual → secret adultery; Moon + Saturn in Lagna → open secret

**8th House (Death, inheritance, sex, occult)**
- War questions: Planet in ASC in Musaripha with Moon → querent wins
- Moon in Muthasila with planet in 7th → enemy wins
- Lord of ASC + Lord of 7th in Musaripha + one combust → war drags on

**9th House (Travel, higher education, law, religion)**
- Lord of ASC in Kendra + Muthasila with Lord of 9th → ✅ will travel
- Lord of 9th in ASC + Ithasala with Lord of ASC → no travel
- Malefics in 7th → travel object fails
- Malefics in 10th → failure due to government/elders
- **Return from exile**: Lord of ASC in Muthasila with planet in ASC or 10th → returns safely
- Lord of ASC in 7th/4th → returns with difficulty

**10th House (Career, reputation, authority)**
- Lord of ASC or Moon in ASC + Muthasila with Lord of 10th → inherits position
- Lord of ASC + Lord of 10th in mutual interchange → gain without effort
- Slow planet afflicted in 4th → reputation at risk
- Fast planet + slow planet strong → position secured

**11th House (Friends, hopes, gains)**
- Lord of ASC + Lord of 11th in Kendras → friendship
- In Panaparas → intimacy
- In Apoklimas → bosom friendship
- **Missing person return**: Benefics in 4th + 10th → will NOT return (they're settled); Malefics in 4th + 10th → WILL return

**12th House (Expenditure, hidden enemies, confinement)**
- 12th house well aspected → money spent on deserving cause
- 12th afflicted → earnings squandered
- For **enemy strength**: Lord of 12th powerful → querent is powerful
- Lord of 7th powerful → enemy is powerful
