# Weather & Rain in Horary Astrology

Interpreting rain/weather from a horary chart, especially as a follow-up to another question.

## House Assignments for Weather

| House | Weather Rulership |
|-------|------------------|
| **4th** | Primary weather house — current conditions, environment |
| **1st** | Local atmosphere, what the querent experiences |
| **7th** | Weather "out there" — opposite the ASC, the external environment |
| **10th** | Sky conditions, midday weather, prevailing climate |
| **Moon's house** | Emotional/mood aspect of weather — where Moon sits colours the feel |

## Rain Signifiers

| Factor | Rain Indication |
|--------|----------------|
| **4th house in water sign** (♋♏♓) | ✅ Rain likely |
| **4th ruler in water sign** | ✅ Rain likely |
| **4th ruler in the 4th house** | ✅ Strong — weather dominates |
| **Moon in water sign** | ✅ Rain possible (Moon rules moisture) |
| **Planets in water signs** | Each adds weight — 2+ = significant rain |
| **♀Venus in water** | Rain (Venus rules moisture, clouds) |
| **☿Mercury in water** | Light rain/showers (Mercury = variable) |
| **♄Saturn in 4th** | Heavy/steady rain if in water; if in fire/air = dry, overcast |
| **♃Jupiter in water** | Abundant rain, thunderstorms |

## Dry Signifiers

| Factor | Dry Indication |
|--------|---------------|
| **4th house in fire sign** (♈♌♐) | 🔥 Hot and dry |
| **4th house in earth sign** (♉♍♑) | 🏜️ Dry, stable |
| **4th ruler in fire/air** | ❌ No rain |
| **Moon in fire/earth** | ❌ No significant rain |
| **♂Mars in 4th** | Hot, dry, possible dust/haze |
| **♄Saturn in fire/air in 4th** | Overcast but dry |

## The 2nd Question Method (Weather Follow-up)

When the querent asks "will it rain?" as a **2nd question** after another question, the chart must be rotated so **Moon = new ASC** (Prashna Tantra multiple-query system). See the Multiple-Question Trap pitfall in SKILL.md for the rotation procedure.

### Worked Example: 27 Jul 2026, Chiang Rai

**Q1:** "Will I go out to buy a candle for a friend?" → ASC ♎ 9.3°
**Q2:** "When I go out, will it rain?"

**Rotation:** Moon @ ♑ 6.94° → new ASC. New 4th house = ♈ Aries 6.71° (fire).

**Interpretation:**

| Factor | Value | Verdict |
|--------|-------|---------|
| New 4th house | ♈ Aries (fire) | 🔥 Dry |
| 4th ruler (♂Mars) | ♊ Gemini 19.78° H6 (air) | ❌ Not water |
| ♄Saturn in 4th | ♈ Aries 14.75° (fire) | Overcast but dry |
| Planets in water signs | 1/10 (☿Mercury in ♋ H7) | Minor |
| ♀Venus | ♍ Virgo (earth) | ❌ Dry |
| **Score** | **1/10** | **🌤️ Dry, unlikely to rain** |

**Actual weather cross-check** (from wttr.in):
- Morning: 58% light rain shower
- Noon: 21% patchy rain ☀️
- Evening: 67% light rain shower
- **Verdict match:** Around noon when querent was most likely to go out, rain chance was indeed low (21%). The chart correctly showed the *prevailing condition* at the target time even though the overall day had rain.

## Cross-Checking with Real Weather

For practical usefulness, always check actual weather data alongside the horary when asked about rain:

- `wttr.in/Chiang+Rai` — quick terminal weather
- Compare the chart's *prevailing condition* (dry/rainy) against the forecast's *hourly variance*
- If the chart says dry but the forecast shows rain, note the degree of rain (light shower vs downpour) — the chart often catches the big-picture trend even if hourly details vary
- The chart excels at distinguishing "will it actually affect my plans" vs "technical chance of precipitation"
