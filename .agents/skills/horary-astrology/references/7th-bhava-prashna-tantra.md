# 7th Bhava Interpretation — Prashna Tantra

Source: `E:\Boom Project\Knowledge\Astrology-Database\#Articles\prashna-tantra.pdf` (Neelakanta)
Extracted: 27 Jul 2026

## Core Principle

> *"The nature of secretion in the woman will be according to the nature of the sign in the 7th house."*
> — Prashna Tantra, Ch. 2, v. 37-38

The **sign occupying the 7th bhava** determines the physical form, attributes, and features of the spouse/partner. Each sign type (tattva/element) gives distinct physical characteristics.

## Form & Attributes by Sign Element

| Tattva (Element) | Physical Form | Complexion | Temperament |
|------------------|---------------|------------|-------------|
| **🔥 Agni (Fire)** — Mesha, Simha, Dhanu | Medium height, wiry, muscular | Reddish/flushed, warm skin | Impatient, passionate, direct |
| **🌍 Prithvi (Earth)** — Vrishabha, Kanya, Makara | Stocky, sturdy, broad frame | Dark/olive, earthy tone | Patient, steady, stubborn |
| **🌬 Vayu (Air)** — Mithuna, Tula, Kumbha | Tall, lean, lanky, prominent joints | Neither dark nor fair, variable | Adaptable, communicative, indecisive |
| **💧 Jala (Water)** — Karka, Vrishchika, Meena | Plump/curvy, soft features | Fair, radiant, clear | Emotional, intuitive, secretive |

## Ithasala/Muthasila & the 7th (v. 58-59)

Example from text:
> *"In regard to a question about getting a wife: the Ascendant is Virgo and the lord Mercury is in Leo. The lord of the 7th Jupiter is in the 7th. There is no aspect between these two planets. The faster-moving planet [Moon] is placed between Mercury and Jupiter and in aspect with them — transfers light from Mercury to Jupiter. The bride will be secured through the assistance of another person."*

This demonstrates **Nakta Yoga** in a 7th house context — light is transferred by a third, faster planet between two non-aspecting significators.

## Quick Reference for Astrologer's Declaration

When querent asks about spouse/partner based on the 7th bhava:

1. **Check the sign** → describes **form/appearance** (via element: fire=dry, earth=sturdy, air=lean, water=soft)
2. **Check planet in H7** → describes **type/status** of partner
3. **Check Moon in H7** → describes **age**
4. **Check sign type** (Chara/Sthira/Dual) → describes **fidelity/character**
5. **Check Ithasala** between H1 lord and H7 lord → describes **how union happens**
