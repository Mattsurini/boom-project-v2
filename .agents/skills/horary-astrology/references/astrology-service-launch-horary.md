# Horary / Prashna — Timing an Astrology Service Launch

Use when BooM asks when she will be ready to offer astrology readings seriously, whether she should open paid astrology services, or when to soft-launch/full-launch.

## Scope

Keep presets separate:

- **Vedic Prashna:** Sidereal Lahiri, sign/whole-sign emphasis, Tajik/Prashna rules.
- **Western Horary:** Tropical Placidus/Regiomontanus style, classical significators/aspects/dignity.

Do not blend systems silently. If BooM asks “Western, then?” after Vedic, reuse the same question moment/location unless a new fully-formed question is clearly being asked.

## Question mapping

| Matter | Western Horary | Vedic Prashna |
|---|---|---|
| BooM / querent | ASC ruler + Moon | Lagna lord + Moon/Chandra relevance |
| Career/service launch | 10th house ruler | 10th bhava/lord |
| Astrology as a discipline | 9th house + Mercury/Jupiter | 9th bhava, Jupiter/Guru, Mercury/Budha for calculation/consultation |
| Clients/consultations | 7th house | 7th bhava |
| Income/readings sold | 2nd/11th | 2nd/11th bhava |
| Daily work/process | 6th | 6th bhava |

## Interpretation rules

### Western

- Early ASC (<3°) = matter is premature; answer can still be read, but advise soft launch or preparation first.
- 10th lord in 9th = career connected to higher knowledge/astrology/teaching.
- 10th lord weak/fallen = service exists but format/pricing/confidence needs refinement before a big launch.
- Moon applying to significator = development soon.
- Hard applying aspect to Saturn before the significator = confidence/system/blocking step must be handled first.
- Use weeks rather than days when the question asks about becoming “serious/professional” because the matter requires setup, packaging, and reputation.

### Vedic Prashna

- Dwisvabhava/dual Lagna = matter changes with effort; not instant, not blocked.
- Strong 10th lord or 10th lord in own sign = viable as a professional service.
- Lagna lord tied to 8th/9th/occult/higher knowledge = genuine astrology/divination pull, but may require deeper study/behind-the-scenes preparation.
- Moon in Lagna = querent is emotionally invested and sincere.
- Saturn obstruction = system/foundation/confidence delay, not necessarily denial.

## Output shape

1. Preset line.
2. Question moment + location.
3. Radicality/caveats.
4. Significator table.
5. Aspect/development table.
6. Timing split:
   - soft launch / examples / waitlist
   - limited paid opening
   - full professional package
7. Direct advice on what to prepare before launch.
8. Source line.

## Example pattern from session

Question: “How much longer until I can do astrology readings seriously?” at 2026-07-27 16:41:46 ICT, Chiang Rai.

- Vedic Prashna indicated **2–4 weeks / mid-August** for serious opening momentum.
- Western Horary had early Capricorn ASC and weak/fallen 10th significator, so it advised **soft launch mid-August** and **full/professional launch early–mid September**.

Reusable synthesis:

> If Vedic says “ready soon” but Western has early ASC/weak 10th lord, recommend staged launch: soft launch first, full launch after packaging and process are stable.

Do not preserve these dates as future facts; recalculate for future questions.
