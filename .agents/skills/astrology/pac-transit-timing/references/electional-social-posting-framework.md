# Electional + Social Posting Framework for PAC Clips

Use when BooM asks for research-backed rules for choosing upload times, not just today's exact time.

## Practical synthesis

For PAC/social video publishing, combine two layers:

1. **Audience availability layer** — when people are actually online.
   - Social media studies generally point to local midday-to-evening windows, with Instagram/TikTok often performing well after work/school and into early evening.
   - Treat platform studies as a starting prior only; BooM's own IG/TikTok analytics override generic benchmarks.

2. **Electional astrology layer** — quality of the moment.
   - This is electional astrology, not horary. The chart is for the moment of publishing/recording.
   - Electional work is rarely perfect; the practical goal is to avoid the worst factors and choose the best available window.

## Astrology factors for PAC/social posts

Priority order for love/PAC content:

1. **Moon condition**
   - Prefer Moon applying to Venus, Mercury, Jupiter, Neptune, or Uranus by soft aspect.
   - Avoid strong Moon hard aspects to Saturn/Mars unless the topic is intentionally heavy, karmic, angry, or confrontational.
   - Avoid Moon VOC for major launches if possible; okay for editing, drafts, or low-stakes reposts.

2. **Planetary hour**
   - Venus Hour: love, beauty, attraction, relationship PAC.
   - Mercury Hour: hooks, captions, short-form video, comments, clever questions.
   - Jupiter Hour: reach, shares, audience expansion, teaching/spiritual wisdom.
   - Moon Hour: emotional resonance, healing, intuitive readings.
   - Sun Hour: creator visibility and authority.
   - Mars Hour: spicy/taboo/conflict hooks; avoid for soft love readings.
   - Saturn Hour: karmic lessons, boundaries, closure; avoid for light engagement.

3. **Angles of the publish chart**
   - Good for online/social: Gemini, Libra, Aquarius, Sagittarius rising or MC.
   - Gemini MC/ASC = communication and short-form messaging.
   - Libra MC/ASC = love, aesthetics, relationships.
   - Aquarius MC/ASC = online community, algorithmic spread, unusual hooks.
   - Sagittarius MC/ASC = reach, teaching, spiritual framing.

4. **Vedic filter**
   - Avoid Rahu Kalam, Yamaganda, and Gulika for normal soft/love PAC.
   - Rahu/Mars periods can be deliberately used for taboo, toxic-love, secret, or spicy topics.

## Output pattern for research-style answers

When BooM asks for the general method/research:

- Start with the usable framework, not a literature dump.
- Separate `astrology` from `social media data`.
- End with a direct operating rule for BooM's PAC workflow.

Suggested concise close:

> The formula that fits BooM: first pick a window when people are actually online, then filter with Venus/Mercury/Jupiter/Moon Hour + Moon aspect + ASC/MC. For love PAC, priority: Venus Hour > Jupiter Hour > Moon Hour > Mercury Hour.

## Pitfalls

- Do not treat generic platform timing studies as destiny; use them as a baseline and test against BooM's own analytics.
- Do not give exact electional times without explaining what each time is optimizing.
- Do not cast horary for upload timing; this is electional/transit timing.
