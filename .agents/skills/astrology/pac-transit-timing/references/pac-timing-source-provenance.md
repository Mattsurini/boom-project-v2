# PAC Timing Source Provenance

Use this when BooM asks where PAC upload timing rules came from, especially challenges like “where does the information come from”, “where do the Electional rules come from”, “where do the Planetary Hours come from”, or “where does the Vedic filter come from”.

## Core stance

Be explicit: the traditional components are source-backed, but applying them to IG/TikTok/PAC publishing is Turboz's interpretive synthesis plus account analytics.

Do **not** claim any source proves “posting PAC during Venus/Moon/Jupiter hour increases views” unless a platform-specific study was actually checked.

## Source-backed components

| Component | Source category | What it supports |
|---|---|---|
| Electional astrology | Skyscript / John Frawley, *Electional Astrology* article from *The Real Astrology* | Electional astrology is the art of choosing the best moment to act; Moon matters in elections. |
| Planetary hours | Renaissance Astrology, *Planetary Hours and Days* | Planetary hours use the Chaldean order: Saturn → Jupiter → Mars → Sun → Venus → Mercury → Moon; used as a secondary technique in traditional astrology/elections. |
| Vedic/Panchang filters | Drik Panchang daily panchang / Muhurta tradition | Rahu Kalam, Yamaganda, Gulikai/Gulika are listed under inauspicious timings and are normally avoided for clean auspicious work. |
| Social posting practicality | Sprout Social / Hootsuite-style platform timing studies | Timing should respect when the audience is active; recency/early engagement matters. BooM's own analytics override generic studies. |

## Interpretive application to PAC / tarot clips

| Traditional factor | PAC interpretation |

## Citation snippets to remember

- Skyscript / Frawley: electional astrology = choosing the best moment to act.
- Renaissance Astrology: planetary hours are based on the ancient Chaldean order, repeating Saturn, Jupiter, Mars, Sun, Venus, Mercury, Moon.
- Drik Panchang: Rahu Kalam, Yamaganda, Gulikai Kalam appear under inauspicious timings in Panchang outputs.
