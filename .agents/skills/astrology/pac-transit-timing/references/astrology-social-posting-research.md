# Astrology + social posting timing research notes

Use this reference when BooM asks for the method behind choosing upload times for PAC / tarot / astrology clips.

## Practical synthesis

Posting-time selection is best treated as electional astrology plus platform audience behavior:

1. Use platform/audience data to narrow to times people are actually online.
   - General social media studies tend to favor local midday through evening windows, with Instagram/TikTok often performing better after school/work and in early evening.
   - For BooM's PAC content, default practical Thai windows are usually 17:30–21:30 unless analytics say otherwise.
2. Use astrology to choose the quality of the moment inside that practical window.
3. Track real account results after posting: reach, retention, saves, shares, comments in the first 1–3 hours.

## Electional astrology principles for uploading clips

Core concept: an election rarely creates a perfect chart. The practical goal is to avoid the worst conditions and select the best available moment.

Prioritize:
- Moon: public response, emotional flow, audience receptivity.
- Mercury: communication, captions, short-form virality, comments, comprehension.
- Venus: love PAC, beauty, softness, attraction, relational content.
- Jupiter: reach, growth, shares, new audience, optimism.
- ASC/MC: how the clip appears publicly and how it travels.

Avoid for soft/love PAC unless deliberately using heavier energy:
- Moon hard Mars: agitation, drama, impatience, spicy conflict.
- Moon hard Saturn: low response, heaviness, silence, delay.
- Void-of-course Moon: weak follow-through / less momentum.
- Strong 12H emphasis for visibility goals.

Useful ASC/MC signatures:
- Gemini: communication, short clips, captions, questions.
- Libra: love/relationships/aesthetic PAC.
- Aquarius: online community, algorithmic spread, collective audience.
- Sagittarius: wisdom, spiritual framing, broader reach.

## Planetary hour filter

Planetary hours follow the Chaldean order: Saturn → Jupiter → Mars → Sun → Venus → Mercury → Moon.

For PAC posting:
- Venus Hour: best for love, attraction, relationship, soft engagement.
- Jupiter Hour: best for reach, sharing, new viewers, growth topics.
- Moon Hour: best for intuitive, emotional, healing, audience bonding.
- Mercury Hour: best for hooks, comments, short explanations, viral questions.
- Sun Hour: best for creator visibility and confident personal branding.
- Mars Hour: use only for spicy/conflict hooks, toxic love, blunt truth, hot takes.
- Saturn Hour: use only for karma, lessons, boundaries, endings, serious readings.

BooM default priority for love PAC:
Venus Hour > Jupiter Hour > Moon Hour > Mercury Hour.

## Vedic / muhurta filters

Use these as additional practical filters, not as a replacement for the electional chart:
- Avoid Rahu Kalam for normal posting; it can scatter attention or make results erratic.
- Avoid Yamaganda for soft reach; it can feel draining.
- Avoid Gulika unless the topic is karmic, shadowy, taboo, or heavy.
- Rahu/Mars windows can be intentionally chosen for spicy/taboo topics if the hook matches.

## Output guidance

If BooM asks for research/method, give the framework first and keep it actionable. Do not drown her in citations. Mention sources as categories:
- Electional astrology tradition: Moon, significators, planetary hours, ASC/MC.
- Renaissance Astrology / traditional planetary hours: Chaldean order.
- Skyscript / John Frawley style electional principle: avoid worst conditions, choose the best available moment.
- Platform timing data: Hootsuite/Sprout-style social media studies; verify with BooM's own account analytics.

## Verification loop

Recommend keeping a lightweight posting log:
- Date/time posted
- Topic/hook
- Planetary hour
- Moon sign/aspect
- ASC/MC
- Rahu/Yamaganda/Gulika status
- 1h/3h reach, saves, shares, comments

After 10–20 posts, prioritize BooM's real analytics over generic social media timing articles.
