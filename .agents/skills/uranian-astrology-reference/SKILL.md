# Uranian Astrology Reference (Hamburg School)

## The 8 Transneptunian Points

Meanings from Wikipedia, article "Hamburg School of Astrology".

### Cupido (CP) — Love, Art
- **Wikipedia:** Romantic love, marriage (via transit to natal love indicators). Artistic abilities, kinds of partnerships.
- **Keywords:** Love, relationships, bonding, marriage, partnership, social connection, artistic creativity, harmony, beauty
- **Transit:** When Cupido hits a natal planet = opens love/relationships/social/art themes
- **Rx:** Review love, old relationships

### Hades (HA) — Deep Psyche, Healing
- **Wikipedia:** Similar to Pluto. Esoteric vocations, healing vocations. Emotional/mental issues, life wounds.
- **Keywords:** Death, endings, the unconscious, secrets, obsession, healing, the underworld, psychological depth
- **Transit:** When Hades hits a natal planet = need to let go, heal, turn inward to spirit
- **Rx:** Hidden things are being revealed

### Zeus (ZE) — Creativity, Success
- **Wikipedia:** Creativity, ambitious achievement. Symbol of fertility.
- **Keywords:** Power, authority, ambition, creative achievement, mastery, kingship, fertility (ideas/projects)
- **Transit:** When Zeus hits a natal planet = creativity surges, strong momentum
- **Rx:** Review goals/ambition

### Kronos (KR) — Authority, Elders, Systems
- **Wikipedia:** Authority, things that are "high up" — heaven, kings, leaders, sky, etc.
- **Keywords:** Father figures, authority figures, discipline, structure, career success, rulership, government, establishment
- **Transit:** When Kronos hits a natal planet = tested by authority/systems, recognition from above
- **Rx:** Review authority, relationships with elders

### Apollon (AP) — Wisdom, Balance
- **Wikipedia:** Wisdom throughout life, balanced temperament, prophetic dreams.
- **Keywords:** Wisdom, balance, prophecy, intuition, business, commerce, expansion, worldly success
- **Transit:** When Apollon hits a natal planet = wisdom/deep understanding or worldly success
- **Rx:** Review success

### Admetos (AD) — Inner Strength, Limitations
- **Wikipedia:** Transformative power (similar to Hades but internal, hard to release outward). Spiritual blockage, depth of knowledge.
- **Keywords:** Inner transformation, restriction, endurance, perseverance, delay, karma, depth, spiritual blockage
- **Transit:** When Admetos hits a natal planet = held back from within, need deep self-understanding
- **Rx:** Review inner limitations

### Vulkanus/Vulcan (VU) — Breaking Through Obstacles
- **Wikipedia:** Overcoming lifetime handicaps (like Hephaestos exiled from Olympus who returned). Brute force of masculine energy wanting freedom.
- **Keywords:** Breakthrough, overcoming obstacles, sudden change, explosive energy, freedom, genius, crisis to transformation
- **Transit:** When Vulkanus hits a natal planet = break through big obstacles, turn crisis into opportunity
- **Rx:** Change is delayed/channelled

### Poseidon (PO) — Spirituality, Universal Love
- **Wikipedia:** Spirituality, similar to Neptune. Positive: spiritual, enlightened, agapical love. Psychic abilities, spiritual wisdom.
- **Keywords:** Spirituality, mysticism, enlightenment, unconditional love, transcendence, psychic ability, higher consciousness, also delusion/deception
- **Transit:** When Poseidon hits a natal planet = open a spiritual dimension, or be dominated by illusion
- **Rx:** Retreat into spirit, meditate
