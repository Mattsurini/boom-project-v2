## Aspect Strength
- **Conjunction (0°)** = strongest, most direct
- **Opposition (180°)** = external conflict, projection
- **Square (90°)** = tension, forced growth
- **Trine (120°)** = easy flow, talent
- **Sextile (60°)** = opportunity
- **Semisextile (30°)** = subtle activation
- **Quincunx (150°)** = adjustment needed

## House Significance
- **Angular (1/4/7/10):** Strongest effect, public/visible
- **Succedent (2/5/8/11):** Moderate, material/relational
- **Cadent (3/6/9/12):** Inner processing, spiritual
