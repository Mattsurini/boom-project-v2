# Uranian Points — Quick Reference for Transit Readings

## The 8 Hamburg Hypothetical Planets

| Point | Stellium Desc | Wikipedia Meaning | Transit Trigger |
|:------|:--------------|:------------------|:----------------|
| **Cupido** ♥ | Relationships, marriage, family | Romantic love, marriage, artistic abilities, partnerships | Opens love/social/art themes |
| **Hades** 💀 | Hidden, past, decay, shadow | Esoteric/healing vocations, emotional/mental issues, life wounds | Let go/heal/turn inward to spirit |
| **Zeus** 👑 | Creativity, fire, machines, directed energy | Creativity, ambitious achievement, fertility | Creativity surges/has momentum |
| **Kronos** ⏳ | Authority, leadership, high position | Authority, "high up" things (heaven, kings, leaders) | Tested by elders/systems |
| **Apollon** 📈 | Science, commerce, expansion/success | Wisdom, balanced temperament, prophetic dreams | Success/wisdom/expansion |
| **Admetos** 🧱 | Depth, raw material, endurance/obstacles | Transformative power (internal), spiritual blockage, depth | Held back within/need to understand self |
| **Vulkanus** 💥 | Power, force, mighty strength | Overcoming handicaps, masculine freedom-energy | Break through obstacles/breakthrough/crisis→opportunity |
| **Poseidon** 🌊 | Spirituality, enlightenment, truth | Spirituality, enlightened love, psychic abilities | Spirituality/illusion/transcendence |

| Transit Scenario | Meaning |
|:-----------------|:--------|

| Mars + Zeus | Creative drive, ambition ignited |
