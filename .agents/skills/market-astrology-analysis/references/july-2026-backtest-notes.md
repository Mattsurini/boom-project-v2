## Practical interpretation

### Nikkei / Japan
Use Vedic/Nakshatra first:
- Moon in Ashlesha / Mula / Ardra = danger / avoid.
- Gandanta = high reversal risk.
- Moon hard Rahu/Ketu = trap/black-swan/large-move warning.
- Multiple negative Moon aspects = caution/no-trade.

### US indices
Use hybrid:
- Price action defines direction.
- Vedic danger can veto entries.
- Western/Uranian flags volatility/reversal risk.

### Gold
Do not use astrology alone for direction. Add:
- DXY
- US yields / real yields
- Price zones and VWAP/structure
- COMEX/New York timing
- News calendar

Gold timing can use planetary hours, but only with chart confirmation.

## Reporting template

For future user requests, answer in this shape:

1. **Asset / date / timezone**
2. **Current price + macro snapshot** if available
3. **Bias:** Bull / Bear / Sideways / No-trade
4. **Risk:** Low / Medium / High
5. **Timing windows:** best and avoid windows with Thai time conversion when relevant
6. **Confirmation:** what price must do
7. **Invalidation:** exact level/condition that cancels the idea

Keep it concise and practical.
