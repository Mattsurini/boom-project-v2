# Neptune Retrograde — Interpretation Notes

Short research note for transit/pick-a-card timing discussions.

## Astronomical fact
- Neptune retrograde must be verified from ephemeris speed/retrograde flags, not guessed from intuition.
- Outer planets are often retrograde for long periods, so the retrograde state is a modifier, not the whole story.

## Source-backed interpretive themes
### Astrology.com
- Neptune retrograde is subtle and can slowly strip away illusions.
- It can reveal reality that was previously masked.
- The effect is often internal and gradual rather than dramatic.

### Astrology King
- Neptune retrograde internalizes fears/anxieties.
- It can surface illusions, false realities, confusion, and old emotional material.
- The tone is inward, reflective, and reality-checking.

## Safe usage language
Use phrases like:
- "may internalize"
- "can feel more reflective"
- "tends to highlight"
- "symbolically fits"

Avoid:
- "definitely means"
- "always good/bad"
- "this alone determines the timing"

## For content timing
- Do not use Neptune retrograde alone to pick an electional slot.
- Treat Moon/Venus/Mercury aspects as the primary timing signal.
- Treat Neptune retrograde as a tone filter: dreamy, introspective, ambiguous, inward.
