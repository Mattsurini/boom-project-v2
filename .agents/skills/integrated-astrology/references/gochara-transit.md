# Gochara (Transit) — H.R. Seshadri Iyer

**Location:** `#Articles/new-techniques-of-prediction.pdf` in ASTROLOGY-BOOKS-DATABASE

## Three-Factor Timing

> "Rice or wheat as it is, is not useful unless it is properly cooked. Likewise a Yoga visible in radix chart may not fructify in his lifetime unless its Dasha intervenes and a favorable transit of that Lord occurs."

Three factors linked together:
1. **Radix position** (birth chart promise)
2. **Dasha** (wide span of years)
3. **Gochara** (removes ambiguities, gives finishing touch to timing)

## Iyer's Method: Lagna-Based Transit

Do NOT use Chandra (Moon) or Surya (Sun) as transit base. Use **Lagna** as basis. Supported by Slokas 34–38, Chapter XX of Phala Deepika.

Apply primarily to **Dasha Lord** (by "Graha" in transit, he means Dasha Lord).

## The 15 Principles of Gochara

### (01) Inherent trait at birth is PRIMARY
- A Graha of bad trait at birth cannot give good effects even transiting favorable positions.
- A Graha good at birth may not give out its good effects while transiting bad position but never does harm even in such bad positions.

### (02) See the Bhava transited (not just Rasi)
Differentiate **Bhava from Rasi** — a Rasi may contain 2-3 Bhava ranges. A planet at 0°–19.5° of a sign may be in one Bhava while 19.5°–30° may be a different Bhava.

- **(a)** A Graha good at birth transiting good Bhavas gives good effects of: the Bhavas it owns at birth + the Bhava it transits or aspects in transit.
- **(02a)** A Graha **approaching** a cuspal degree gives formative/developing effects.
- **(02b)** A Graha **receding** from a cuspal point gives fading/diminishing effect.
- **(02c)** At the **exact cuspal point** — maximum effect of that Bhava.
- **(b)** A Graha good at birth transiting **Dustanas** (6, 8, 12) — remains neutral, doesn't show good qualities.

### (03) Nature of the Rasi transited
- **(a)** Good Graha in own/exaltation/friendly sign → good. In debilitation/enemy's sign → remains neutral.
- **(b)** Bad Graha in debilitation/enemy's house/Zero Rasis → neutral (but never good). In other signs → may prove bad.

### (04) Nakshatra transited
- **(a)** Good Graha transiting the Nakshatra of: a good (functional) Graha, Birth Yogi, Dasha Yogi, Dasha Raja Yogi → gives good. Otherwise → bad.
- **(b)** Per mathematical Navamsha chart, see if Graha transits any of three sensitive stars: the star where the Graha is situated, the star to which it gives Amsha, the star at radix position.

### (05) Combustion, Retrogression, Speed
- **(a)** **Combust Graha**: Totally ineffective if good at birth; gives worst effect if bad at birth.
- **(b)** **Retrograde**: Very powerful for the moment (good or bad depending on birth trait).
- **(c)** **Fast moving (Athichara)**: Likely gives very dangerous effect.
- **(d)** **Slow moving (Manda)**: Slow in action.
- **(e)** **Stagnant (Sthambana)**: Neutral in effect.

### (06) Zero Rasis (Dagdha/burnt signs)
- **(a)** Positive Grahas transiting non-Zero Rasis → good. In Zero Rasis → submerged unless retrograde.
- **(b)** Negative Grahas and Avayogi → good while transiting Zero Rasis unless retrograde.
- **(c)** Rahu and Ketu transiting Zero Rasis → good.

### (07) Conjunctions and aspects during transit
If a transiting Graha is joined/aspected by friendly benefic → happy results. Otherwise → bad.

### (08) Treat birth Yogi similar to Dasha Lord
Birth Yogi transit is subsidiary to major Lord transit.

### (09) Cuspal transits
A Graha transiting **exact cuspal points** gives timely effects. Order of importance: **10th cusp > Lagna > 4th > 7th**. Other cusps may not be effective for transit.

### (10) Consider Bhukti Lord Gochara
Optionally, consider Gochara of Bhukti Lord also.

### (11) Thithi, Weekday, Nakshatra, Yoga, Lagna at time of events
Events should happen in Thithi, Weekday, Nakshatra, Yoga or Lagna of Dasha Lord/Bhukti Lord/Anthara Lord (according to powers).

Find **ruler of Dasha Lord**. Good effects happen when **Yoga Graha synchronizes** with this ruler. Also when Dasha Lord passes through this ruler's Nakshatra. When **two or more** of above elements are satisfied → that day is certain.

### (12) Progression of Chandra (Secondary Directions)
For quick-moving Dasha Lords (Sun, Mercury, Venus): they circle zodiac many times in Dasha period.
For slow-moving ones (Jupiter, Saturn, Rahu, Ketu): span in sign/nakshatra is wide.

**Solution**: Use progression of Chandra (western secondary directions). Progressed Chandra gives effect of Bhava and Nakshatra it transits similarly to Dasha Lord.

### (13) Western Progression Research
Meridian progression to planets and planetary progression to meridian. Even here, allow ~1 year interval.

## Key Takeaways for Daily Transit Work

1. **Lagna-based transit** is preferred over Chandra-based — use local transit ASC, not natal Moon position
2. **Look at Bhava transited, not just Rasi** — Venus in house 1 (Bhava 1) is powerful even if it's technically in Virgo
3. **Approaching cusp = formative effect** — Venus approaching the ASC gives developing opportunities
4. **Retrograde = powerful for the moment** — check speed before interpreting transit
5. **Cusp priority**: 10th > ASC > 4th > 7th for meaningful effects
