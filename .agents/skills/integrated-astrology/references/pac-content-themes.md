## 📝 How to use this guide

1. Check current transit — what's the Moon sign? Any New/Full Moon? Any Rx?
2. Pick the theme that matches the astro event from tables above
3. Adjust wording to your personal style
4. Pair with `references/pick-a-card-timing.md` for the best shooting time

**Example today (Jul 13-14, 2026):**
- New Moon in ♋ Cancer → "What's coming home to your heart 🌑"
- Mercury Rx in ♋ Cancer → "Messages from family / ex that you need to hear"
- Combine: "New Moon in Cancer — What your past wants you to know & what's coming next"
