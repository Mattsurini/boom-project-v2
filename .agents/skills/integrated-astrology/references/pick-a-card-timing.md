# Pick A Card / Tarot Content — Electional Timing Guide

Published **2026-07-13** by Turboz for BooM's content calendar.

## Core Factors for Publishing Intuitive/Tarot Content

| Factor | Weight | Why |
|--------|:------:|-----|
| **☽ Moon sign & aspects** | 🟢 High | Intuition, emotional connection, viewer resonance |
| **☿ Mercury aspects** | 🟢 High | Clarity of message, communication flow |
| **♀ Venus aspects** | 🟡 Medium | Aesthetics, visual appeal, beauty of the video |
| **♆ Neptune aspects** | 🟡 Medium | Spiritual connection, psychic attunement |
| **Avoid heavy □ ♄ Saturn** | 🔴 Critical | Feels blocked, heavy, too serious for light content |

## Session Note: Love PAC Timing — Chiang Rai, 2026-07-27

For a love Pick A Card upload, the best window found in this session was **12:15–12:45 ICT** when the local chart had:
- Moon in a communicative house (3rd)
- Sun + Jupiter in the 10th for visibility
- Mercury still supporting message flow
- Venus active without the later Sun–Pluto heaviness

Avoid pushing the upload past the early afternoon if the goal is an easy, accessible love reading; the post-13:30 window becomes heavier and more intense.
