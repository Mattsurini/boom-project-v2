# Insight: Japanese Divination = Agency-Preserving, Love-Driven, Multi-Tier

## Core insight / verdict
Japanese fortune-telling is a **spectrum across 4 registers**, not one system: (1) casual/ambient — Western zodiac TV ranking, blood type, rokuyō lucky-days on calendars; (2) shrine ritual — omikuji/koimikuji, omamori, enkiri power-spots; (3) systematic East-Asian calculation — shichūsuimei (BaZi JP), kyūsei kigaku (Nine Star Ki), onmyōdō/fūsui; (4) personal direct — palm (tesō), kanji name analysis (seimei handan), tarot.

The unifying function: **agency-preserving reflection, not fatalistic prediction**. "How you accept it and move forward is up to you." Good → motivation, bad → caution. Even self-declared non-believers are behaviorally nudged (PMC9451074). Love/relationships dominate consultations (especially young women).

## Evidence class
- external web research (taxonomy); peer-reviewed superstition study (mechanism); cultural essays (frameworks). Prevalence claims = qualitative, NOT statistical.

## Practical / PAC usage
- Excellent cultural framing for Japanese-flavored divination content (omikuji/koimikuji, rokuyō as day-auspice, Nine Star Ki).
- Reinforces BooM's writing rule: mechanism-over-label, agency preserved, love-driven, client-paced.
- Borrow the "reflection prompt, not verdict" tone for client readings.

## Do / Don't
- DO: present as cultural spectrum; keep 4 registers distinct; use agency-preserving framing.
- DON'T: call any method "#1"/most-used as a statistic; merge shrine-lot luck with calculational systems; read Japanese divination as fatalistic.
