## Chart Describes Terrain; Soulmate is Built

**The Golden Thread:** Vedic astrology's marriage machinery (event + character + compatibility) genuinely overlaps with what psychology shows predicts relationship durability — but the "soulmate" experience is the *belief system* layered on top, and that belief amplifies whichever direction the union is already leaning.

**Convergence mechanics:**
1. Astrology provides the **terrain** — marriage timing (BPHS Ch.18), marriage character (Navamsa/D-9, Darakaraka), and compatibility dimensions (36 gunas: temperament, mental wavelength, constitution).
2. Psychology provides the **amplifier** — the soulmate belief makes outcomes fit-dependent (Franiuk 2004): a strong chart + ideal-perceived partner + soulmate belief = maximum satisfaction; the same belief with a poor-fit partner = enhanced conflict and higher divorce (Wilcox & Dew 2010).
3. The honest synthesis: astrology identifies whether marriage is strongly supported and where friction will live; the lasting bond is built through effort, fit-awareness, and commitment — not decreed by fate.

**Critical warning:** The claim "astrology identifies YOUR soulmate" outruns the evidence on BOTH sides — no classical text distinguishes a destined bond from a merely well-supported marriage, and psychology shows destiny-framing is double-edged. Readings that promise a fated person risk amplifying future conflict when perceived fit fails. Always deliver the three-layer framing: event → character → compatibility, with the boundary "marriage ≠ happiness" made explicit.
