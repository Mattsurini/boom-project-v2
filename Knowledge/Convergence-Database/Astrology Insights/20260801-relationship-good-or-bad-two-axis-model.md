## The Good-or-Bad Two-Axis Model (Terrain × Skill — Not a Verdict)

**The Golden Thread:** The chart supplies the **terrain** (structural tension); psychology decides **survival** (climate — skill & fit). Astrology can describe where a relationship will strain and when; it cannot deliver a verdict on whether it is "good" or "bad." The honest model is two axes, not a score.

**Convergence mechanics — the two-axis model:**
1. **Axis 1 — Structural Tension (from the chart).** Legible, shloka-grounded, cancellable. *Low tension:* benefic 7th baseline, dignified/benefic 7th lord, clean D-9, benefic 2nd-from-UL, cancellable afflictions → terrain that favors ease. *High tension:* malefic 7th baseline, 7th lord in 6/8/12 or debilitated/combust, afflicted D-9, malefic 2nd-from-UL, uncancelled afflictions, Papa Kartari → terrain that demands work: more rupture cycles, more timing pressure, higher repair demand. Tension is a description of *difficulty*, never a prediction of *doom*.
2. **Axis 2 — Relational Skill & Fit (from behavior and psychology).** *High capacity:* Growth ("work-it-out") beliefs, a working 5:1 positive-to-negative ratio, active repair after rupture, cognitive interdependence ("we" identity), attachment security. *Low capacity:* Destiny/soulmate beliefs with low fit, breakdown of the 5:1 ratio (≤1:1), failed repair attempts, contempt, avoidant collapse under strain.
3. **The two governing claims.** (a) **The chart fixes Axis 1, not Axis 2** — astrology is competent at structural tension and its timing, not at measuring relational skill; skill, not tension, is what the verified psychology says decides survival. (b) **Outcome = tension × capacity, with the frame deciding the sign.** High tension + high skill = a demanding but **developable** bond (the growth-believers' catalyst). High tension + low skill = the relationship that **feels bad and often is**. Low tension + low capacity = stays but may not thrive. The chart cannot promise outcomes; the destiny/growth frame decides whether a hard transit is "proof it wasn't meant to be" or a catalyst.

**The genuine convergences (structure and framing, not causal identity):**
- Afflictions = high-structure terrain = high repair demand — the layers agree on *difficulty*, not *outcome*.
- The destiny/growth frame decides what a "bad transit" *is* — the chart times the strain; the belief system assigns its meaning. The cleanest two-layer alignment.
- Event ≠ quality = duration ≠ thriving — the classical separation and the psychological separation are the same distinction in two languages.
- The honeymoon fade maps onto D-1 "trailer" vs. D-9 "lived reality" (analogy with Gander 2024 trajectories and Evans 2026 limerence fade — illustrates, does not prove).
- Relationship-as-entity is a shared structural metaphor (UL / composite / we-ness) — convergence at the level of the metaphor, not the mechanism.

**Critical warning:** Rejected as convergences — positive-aspect toxicity as a *causal* astro finding; Mars–Moon → fearful-avoidant as causal; Rahu ≈ Neptune; composite = UL = third-entity as corroboration. The scorecard weights (25/20/30/25) and any ≥80% separation claim are **heuristic, not doctrine**. Correlation status: astrology-layer predictive correlation is **unvalidated**; psychology-layer climate factors are **well-validated**; the cross-layer link is a **well-formed hypothesis awaiting the B1-B4 studies**.

**Open research gaps (the honest agenda):**
1. **DAS-validated outcome dataset (B1):** charts mapped to Spanier's Dyadic Adjustment Scale (Spanier 1976, pp. 15-28) + divorce/longevity records across cultures — prerequisite for any weight or separation claim.
2. **Growth-belief × bad-synastry interaction test (B2):** Knee's ITR subscales × a specific "bad" signature (e.g., Mars-square-Moon) × DAS + longevity — the test that could genuinely limit or support the astrological claim.
3. **Gottman-ratio × aspect correlation (B3):** SPAFF-coded conflict (5:1 measurement) crossed with natal aspect signatures.
4. **Subjective natal-needs weighting (B4):** individual-difference conflict-tolerance / need-for-intensity variables folded into the B2/B3 designs — "bad" is partly relative to the native's tolerance (Gottman "volatile" style is functional).

**Application:** Read the terrain to know where the relationship will strain and when; read the couple's skills and beliefs to know whether that terrain will be survived. A "good" relationship on paper can be a "bad" lived one, and vice versa. Never produce a numeric verdict — deliver the two-axis model: structural tension × relational skill.
