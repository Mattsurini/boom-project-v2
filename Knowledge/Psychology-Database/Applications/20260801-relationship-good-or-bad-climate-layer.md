## The Good-or-Bad Relationship Climate Layer (Attachment · Beliefs · We-ness · Repair)

The psychology layer is the **outcome-deciding half**: belief systems and interaction skills — not static trait matches and not chart signatures — are the *measured* correlates of relationship survival. Every construct below is independently verified against the published record; the astrology→psychology mappings are not.

**The golden thread (verified climate factors):**
1. **Attachment collapse layer — Internal Working Models reframe afflictions.** A "harsh"/malefic chart signature is re-read as an attachment-pattern expression, not a moral verdict. The "simultaneously desires and terrifies closeness" pattern is the **Fearful-Avoidant** quadrant (Bowlby 1973/1982; Hazan & Shaver 1987; **Bartholomew 1990, *JSPR* 7(2):147-178**; Main & Solomon 1990). *No empirical study links any chart signature to any attachment style — no chart signature is a psychological diagnosis.*
2. **Destiny vs. Growth beliefs (Knee).** Destiny/soulmate believers interpret conflict as proof the relationship "wasn't meant to be" and collapse under "bad" transits; Growth ("work-it-out") believers use the same events as catalysts. Growth believers show no fit-based bias; destiny believers show strong fit-based bias. Verified: **frames change outcomes** (Knee 1998; Knee et al. 2001; Franiuk et al. 2002/2004; **Franiuk et al. 2012, *JSPR* 29(6):820-838**; **Gander et al. 2024, *European Journal of Personality*, N=904 couples** — destiny → higher initial satisfaction, growth → slower decline).
3. **Sovereign Us / cognitive interdependence (Agnew).** The relationship operates as a unit above the two individuals: plural-pronoun ("we") language, self-other overlap (IOS), and relationship centrality track commitment (**Agnew, Van Lange, Rusbult & Langston 1998**; Aron, Aron & Smollan 1992; Karan, Rosenthal & Robbins 2019, 30-study we-talk meta-analysis). **The "third entity with its own destiny" is a metaphor, not a fated claim** — we-ness predicts functioning, not a fixed fate.
4. **Rupture & repair + 5:1 ratio (Gottman).** Durable couples maintain ~5:1 positive-to-negative interactions during conflict; ≤1:1 predicts divorce risk. Perpetual problems are managed (empathy, humor, attunement), not eliminated. A high-tension relationship only collapses into a "bad" outcome if partners lack repair tools (Gottman & Levenson "magic ratio"; UW News 2004 94% divorce-prediction model; Gottman & Krokoff 1989 — conflict *engagement* predicts longitudinal improvement).
5. **Shadow work / individuation.** Infatuation/"recognition" is partly internal projection of the anima/animus; withdrawal of projection onto a real, separate partner is the individuation task (Jung 1925 / *CW 16*; established clinical framework, no outcome data).
6. **Positive-aspect toxicity.** The most toxic relationships can have extremely positive aspects — positive-chart signals predict *staying*, not *thriving*; attraction and escalation can coexist with high-conflict function (Wilcox & Dew 2010 high-satisfaction + high-conflict profile; Gottman "volatile" style; **Evans et al. 2026, *Acta Psychologica* Vol 267, article 107043, N=1647** — intensity/limerence tracks insecurity and ACEs, not durability; Dutton & Painter 1993). **(Practitioner assertion; verified only that intensity + conflict co-occur.)**

**Critical warning:** The astrology→psychology linkages (Mars–Moon → fearful-avoidant; Saturn-in-7th → endurance; D-1/D-9 → trajectory; 2nd-from-UL → "consistent breakdown") are **illustrative and unmeasured** — [PARTIAL]→[SPECULATIVE], never causal or diagnostic. The verified half is the psychology; the astrological half remains untested against the validated instruments (DAS, Gottman interaction coding, ITR scales).

**Application:** In a "good or bad relationship" reading, deliver the climate layer alongside the terrain: assess attachment working models (fear-of-closeness vs. desire-for-closeness), belief system (destiny vs. growth), we-ness/cognitive interdependence, and the 5:1 rupture-repair ratio. The belief system decides what a "bad transit" *means* — Destiny reads it as proof it wasn't meant to be; Growth reads it as a catalyst. Encourage growth beliefs and repair skills; never fate-dependence.
