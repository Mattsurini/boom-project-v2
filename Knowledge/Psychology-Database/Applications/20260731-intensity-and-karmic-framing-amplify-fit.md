## Intensity & "Karmic/Fated" Framing Amplify Fit-Dependence

Intensity and a fated/"karmic" framing of a bond are belief-system amplifiers that magnify whichever direction the union is already leaning — they do NOT predict durability.

- **Intensity predicts insecurity, not durability.** The "fated one" rush (limerence) correlates with insecure attachment and adverse childhood experiences — a trauma/attachment signal often misread as cosmic destiny. (Evans et al., 2026, *Acta Psychologica* **267**:107043, N=1,647)
- **Crisis-bonding ≠ durability.** Trauma/crisis bonding is real (intermittent-reinforcement model, Dutton & Painter, 1993) but is *not* evidence the bond will last — soulmate-model marriages report high satisfaction AND high conflict AND higher divorce. (Wilcox & Dew, 2010, N=1,414)
- **Karmic framing is a meaning-system.** The Rnanubandha ("karmic debt-bond") concept functions as a destiny-belief cognition (overlapping Knee, 2001). Its endurance-incentivizing pole is plausible; the debt-as-motive-to-stay pole is unmeasured and clinically caution-worthy. It is an interpretation, not evidence.
- **Secrecy confers no durability bonus.** Concealment is neutral-to-negative for relationship quality and commitment support — a valid *formation-context* signal, not a stability factor. (Lehmiller, 2009)
- **Soulmate is built, not fated.** The stable high-quality model is institutional/religious-companionate hybrid; work-it-out (growth) beliefs show NO fit-based bias at all. (Wilcox & Dew, 2010; Franiuk et al., 2004)

**Application:** When counseling a client who "feels" fated to an intense partner, validate the feeling but separate the marriage-event question (timing/character) from the belief-system question. Prescribe safety, not intensity: crisis-bonding and limerence are signals to *assess fit and attachment*, not cosmic confirmation. Encourage fit-awareness and relationship-building (work-it-out), never fate-dependence — the belief amplifies whatever the union actually is.
