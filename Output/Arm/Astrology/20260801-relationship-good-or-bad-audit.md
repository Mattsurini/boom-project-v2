Likely cause: the `.doc` text omits a SANKETA SIX header at that boundary, so the house-lords block (sl.15-16) was absorbed into SANKETA FOUR by the extractor.

**FC-2 (psychology).** *Incorrect:* Bartholomew (1990) "Avoidance of Intimacy: An Attachment Perspective" cited as *JPSP* 58:644-651 (§4 and §6). *Correct:* published in **Journal of Social and Personal Relationships, 7(2), 147-178**, doi:10.1177/0265407590072001. The four-category/fearful-avoidant substance is correct; journal metadata is wrong.

**FC-3 (psychology).** *Incorrect:* Franiuk, Shain, Bieritz & Murray (2012) listed under journal "Personal Relationships" (§4 and §6) with doi:10.1177/0265407512444374. *Correct:* the DOI prefix 026540751 is **Journal of Social and Personal Relationships**; the paper is *JSPR* 29(6), 820-838 (N=143). Substance (soulmate × poor fit: early protection, lost over time) verified accurate.

**FC-4 (psychology).** *Incorrect:* "recent N=904-couple dyadic growth-curve study (PMC13029515, **2026**)" and "PMC 2026 review" (§3, §4). *Correct:* the study is **Gander, Uhlich, Traut, Saameli, Bühler, Weidmann & Grob (2024)**, "The role of relationship beliefs in predicting levels and changes of relationship satisfaction," **European Journal of Personality**, doi:10.1177/08902070241240029, N=904 couples, 2 years, 4 waves (PMC13029515). **FC-5 (psychology).** *Incorrect:* Spanier (1976) pages "38(1), 15–38" (§6). *Correct:* **Journal of Marriage and the Family, 38(1), 15–28** (doi:10.2307/350547). DOI, 32-item and four-subscale descriptions verified correct.
