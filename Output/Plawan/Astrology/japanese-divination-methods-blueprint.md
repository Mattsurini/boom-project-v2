# Blueprint: Japanese Divination Methods (uranai 占い)

## Scope
Culture/methodology research on how people in Japan routinely engage with fortune-telling — casual and serious forms, systems used, and the underlying attitude. NOT natal-chart calculation. Focuses on method taxonomy + everyday usage + psychology. Strict scope: Japan only; exclude Chinese/Korean systems except where they were imported (I Ching, feng shui, onmyōdō) as Japanese practice.

## Guiding questions (JSON in questions.json)
1. What are the main divination systems Japanese people actually use (taxonomy + medium)?
2. Which are everyday/casual vs. serious consultation?
3. How is divination institutionalized (shrines/temples, TV, salons, online)?
4. What beliefs/attitudes underpin use? (casual entertainment → sincere consultation)
5. How should a foreign astrologer position such methods relative to Western/Vedic practice?
