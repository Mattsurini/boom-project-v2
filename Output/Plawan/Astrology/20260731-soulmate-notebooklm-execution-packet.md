# 🚀 NOTEBOOKLM EXECUTION PACKET — "Vedic Astrology and Finding Soulmate"

Copy the **Context Note** below into a NotebookLM Note. Then run the **Prompt Sequence** one at a time. Finish with the **Blind-Spot Protocol**.

---

## 1️⃣ THE "CONTEXT NOTE" (upload as a Note)

You are a research analyst with mastery of classical Jyotisha (Vedic astrology) and a critical, evidence-first mindset. You are helping test the hypothesis: *"Vedic astrology can reliably fingerprint a soulmate-level bond through the confluence of (1) a strong, well-disposed 7th house/7th lord + Venus, (2) a dignified Darakaraka in the Navamsa, (3) synergistic Moon-nakshatra placements between partners, and (4) a marriage-signifying dasha window."*

While answering, keep in mind:
- Distinguish **marriage** (social contract) from **soulmate** (deep karmic resonance) — they are not the same.
- Weight dignity, dasha, and Navamsa together. No single "perfect aspect" proves anything.
- Quote shlokas/chapters when possible; if a claim is NOT in the sources, say "NOT IN SOURCES" explicitly rather than inventing it.
- You are allowed — and encouraged — to flag weaknesses in the astrological premise itself.

---

## 2️⃣ THE "PROMPT SEQUENCE"

### Phase 1 — Establishing Facts (baseline, non-adversarial)
1. From the uploaded texts, extract the definitive classical combinations (yogas) for marriage quality and spouse character. Quote shloka/chapter references where present.
2. Explain how the 7th house (spouse), the 7th lord (marriage itself), and Venus (karaka of desire/marriage) are meant to be weighted together, and how the texts resolve conflicts between their dignities.
3. List the classical rules for timing marriage — 7th-lord dasha, navamsa dispositor, Sun/Moon transits — and rank them by priority as the texts do.

*Why:* This locks the authoritative baseline so later answers are grounded, not invented.
*High-quality answer looks like:* Exact yogas with chapter references; a clear hierarchy of rules; named conflicts and how tradition resolves them.

### Phase 2 — Testing the Hypothesis (the core probes)
4. What makes a 7th house / Venus "afflicted"? Is affliction always negative, or do some placements produce stabilizing strength (e.g., Saturn in 7th = endurance)? Give the nuance the texts allow.
5. In the Jaimini tradition, what does the **Darakaraka** (lowest-degree planet) reveal about the spouse and the marriage karma, and how does its Navamsa position modify the verdict?
6. Does the evidence support **Nadi / root compatibility** (Moon nakshatra counts 5/7/9) as a predictor of durable harmony? Where is it stated, and with what strength of claim?

*Why:* These three probes directly test markers (2), (3), and (4) of the hypothesis.
*High-quality answer looks like:* Specific planet/house logic with source citations; explicit statements of confidence level; no hand-waving about "spiritual energy."

### Phase 3 — Adversarial / Critical Check (steelman the skeptic)
7. Steelman the case that heavy marriage yogas can still produce a *karmic, unhappy* union — i.e., marriage ≠ happiness. What in the sources supports or refutes this?
8. Are doshas (Mangal, Kaal Sarpa, Shrapit) over-weighted by modern practice compared to the classics? Which texts contain them, which omit them?
9. When D-1 (rashi) and D-9 (navamsa) contradict each other for marriage, how do the classical authorities resolve the contradiction?
10. What would falsify the hypothesis? If two charts show perfect synastry but the marriage never happens, what are the classical explanations — and which of those explanations sound like post-hoc rationalization?

*Why:* This phase is designed to catch confirmation bias and produce the audit log the analysts need later.
*High-quality answer looks like:* Directly refuting or qualifying earlier answers; flagging uncertainty; identifying where the sources are silent.

## 3️⃣ THE "BLIND-SPOT PROTOCOL"

**Run this exact prompt last:**

> Given only the sources provided, list everything MISSING that would be critical to proving whether Vedic astrology can identify a soulmate connection and its timing. Cover: (1) psychological evidence on how partners actually bond (attachment, projection, limerence); (2) any source that questions or refutes the astrological claims; (3) missing classical material on synastry/guna-matching or marriage timing that could change the conclusion; (4) the distinction between cultural/social marriage and genuine soulmate resonance. For each gap, say exactly what evidence or source would close it.

**How to interpret the result:**
- If the AI names a gap in **psychology** (attachment, projection, limerence) → we need to add relationship-psychology sources to the knowledge base, and the psychology track of the analysis must address it.
- If the AI says **NOT IN SOURCES** for a key classical claim → flag that marker for the Astrology-Database acquisition wishlist (mark as `[NOT IN DB]` in the analysis).
- If the AI's "falsification" answers (Phase 3, Q10) sound like post-hoc rationalization → note it as a *critic-level* concern to feed to the Audit phase; do NOT let it be laundered into the final report as verified fact.
