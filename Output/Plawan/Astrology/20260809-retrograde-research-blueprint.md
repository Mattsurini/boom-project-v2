# 💡 IDEA CARD: Retrograde ไม่ใช่แค่ “ถอยหลัง”
**Core Concept:** Research retrograde/vakri as a multi-layer astrological condition: astronomical apparent motion, Vedic cheshta/motional strength, dasha timing reversal, KP filtering, and practical interpretation.

**Objective:** สรุปว่า Retrograde ควรอ่านยังไงให้ไม่ตื้น: มันแรงขึ้น? ให้ผลช้า? ให้ผลย้อนกลับ? หรือควรถูก reject ในบางระบบ?

**Key Angles:**
- Technical Vedic: Vakra as one of 8 planetary motions; cheshta bala / motional strength.
- Timing: Dasha/Drekkana effects are read in reverse order when planet is retrograde.
- Nadi/Bhrigu: Vakri influences previous house while still progressing occupied house, with perplexity.
- KP/Prashna: retrograde ruling planets can be rejected during retrogression.
- Psychological/content: retrograde as review/rework/return pattern, but must be labeled synthesis not classical fact.
