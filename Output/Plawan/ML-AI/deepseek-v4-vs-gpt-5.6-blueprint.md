# Research Blueprint: DeepSeek-V4 vs GPT-5.6

## Scope
Compare the officially documented API offerings, capabilities, context/output limits, pricing, integration surface, and decision fit. Do not infer benchmark superiority without independently verified benchmark methodology and results.

## Questions
1. What exact model variants are being compared?
2. What are the documented context length, maximum output, tools, API compatibility, and reasoning modes?
3. What are the input/output prices and concurrency limits?
4. Which model is the better default for high-complexity reasoning/coding, cost-sensitive volume, and self-directed experimentation?
5. Which claims remain unverified and require controlled task-level evaluation?

## Decision criteria
Capability fit, evidence quality, price, context/output capacity, tool support, deployment/API friction, reproducibility, privacy/compliance, and task-specific quality.
