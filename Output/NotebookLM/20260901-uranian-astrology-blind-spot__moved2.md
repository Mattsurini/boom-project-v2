1. Interpreting the TNOs by House Placement and Natal Conjunctions
To analyze the Transneptunian points (TNOs) in a natal chart, practitioners must understand that Uranian astrology does not view them as independent, standalone bodies
. Instead, they function primarily as "adjectives" that qualify standard personal planets and points
.
Furthermore, house interpretation in this system differs radically from traditional quadrant-based houses (like Placidus)
:
The Meridian House System: The Hamburg School relies primarily on equal 30
∘
segments measured along the celestial equator, with the Midheaven (MC) anchoring the cusp of the 10th house
. In this system, the MC represents the conscious self (the "I" or the soul), while the Ascendant (Asc) floats freely as an auxiliary point representing "others" and the immediate environment
.
Integrative vs. Pure Symmetric Approaches: Historically, Hermann Lefeldt sought to integrate Witte's hypothetical points with traditional 12-house overlays
. Conversely, other purists following Ruth Brummund abandoned house systems entirely, focusing strictly on pure midpoint symmetry and planetary pictures on the dial
.
When applying house and conjunction overlays, here is how each TNO operates:
Cupido (CU)
House Placement: Reveals the area of life where the native seeks social integration, marital union, or group alliances
. In the 10th House (MC), the career is often tied to family businesses, cooperative corporations, or the arts
.
Natal Conjunctions:
Sun/Cupido (SU/CU): Represents the married man, an identity deeply rooted in family ties, or a highly developed aesthetic/artistic self-expression
.
Moon/Cupido (MO/CU): Symbolizes the married woman, a domestic life focused on motherhood, or public-facing artistic activities
.
Node/Cupido (NO/CU): Points to strong family connections, clan relations, or formal entry into corporate/social groups
.
Mars/Cupido (MA/CU): Signifies collaborative group work, shared team projects, or cooperative business alliances
.
Hades (HA)
House Placement: Marks where the native confronts decay, past-life karma, waste, or chronic limitations
. In the 4th House (IC), it can indicate hidden family secrets, antique-filled homes, or early life environments that felt restrictive or impoverished
.
Natal Conjunctions:
Sun/Hades (SU/HA): Indicates physical vulnerability, chronic health challenges, or a life where one must guard deep personal secrets
.
MC/Hades (MC/HA): Indicates an inner self that harbors a profound, dark secret, or a vocational path dedicated to depth analysis, esotericism, or working with raw, decayed matter
.
Asc/Hades (ASC/HA): Describes an immediate, external environment that is dirty, unpleasant, or rustic
.
Moon/Hades (MO/HA): Represents a suffering maternal figure, a widow, or deep subconscious sorrow
.
Venus/Hades (VE/HA): Correlates to a love of antiques, working in supportive service roles (like waitressing), or taboo/secretive sexual desires
.
Mercury/Hades (ME/HA): Points to mental blocks, a tendency to make careless mistakes, or analytical work involving history and the past
.
Zeus (ZE)
House Placement: Details the sector of absolute targeted ambition, mechanical focus, and direct, willful creation
.
Natal Conjunctions:
MC/Zeus or Sun/Zeus (MC/ZE or SU/ZE): Signifies highly disciplined, goal-driven executive ambitions, mechanical/industrial careers, or physical vitality directed toward a singular leadership purpose
.
Venus/Zeus (VE/ZE): Represents high fertility, energetic sexuality, or targeted fertility treatments
.
Kronos (KR)
House Placement: Denotes the area where the native seeks absolute independence, self-determination, and the "highest standard" of excellence
.
Natal Conjunctions:
Sun/Kronos or Moon/Kronos (SU/KR or MO/KR): Represents a president, a highly elevated corporate leader, or interacting directly with high government officials
.
Node/Kronos (NO/KR): Points to connections with people in high places or legal authorities
.
Mars/Kronos (MA/KR): Indicates independent actions, working for oneself, and owning a master business
.
Apollon (AP)
House Placement: Pinpoints the house of exponential growth, commerce, peaceful expansion, and multiplicity of knowledge
.
Natal Conjunctions:
Sun/Apollon or Jupiter/Apollon: Expands physical vitality and triggers massive commercial, educational, or material prosperity
.
Admetos (AD)
House Placement: Indicates the house of crystallization, narrow boundaries, real estate, and deep, introspective stasis
. In the 4th House, it represents property, land ownership, agriculture, or returning to one's primeval birthplace
.
Natal Conjunctions:
Sun/Admetos, MC/Admetos, or Asc/Admetos: Correlates to real-estate investments, property, narrow apartments, or working with raw materials of the earth
.
Venus/Admetos (VE/AD): Signifies blocked love, structural delays in intimacy, or physical hardening of blood vessels (such as arterial calcification when interacting with the Sun/physical body)
.
Vulkanus (VU)
House Placement: Represents the area of life where the native wields overwhelming power, titanic energy reserves, or experiences irreversible fate
.
Natal Conjunctions:
Node/Vulkanus (NO/VU): Signifies highly influential, powerful, and fate-altering collective connections
.
Poseidon (PO)
House Placement: Marks the sector of mental illumination, ideological wisdom, culture, and pure spiritual truth
.
Saturn/Poseidon (SA/PO): Points to a highly disciplined, systematic education or spiritual training
.
Jupiter/Saturn/Poseidon (JU/SA/PO): Often represents physical structures of faith, such as a church, temple, or synagogue
.
2. The Shadow and Negative Manifestations
Because early 20th-century Uranian astrology was heavily influenced by wartime conditions, several classic delineations skewed heavily toward negativity
. However, even in psychological analysis, each point carries a distinct structural "shadow":
Cupido: Rigid structural conformity, the smothering of individual identity within family or corporate group expectations, and forced social assimilation
.
Hades: Severe physical or mental illness, poverty, servitude, exposure of humiliating secrets, and chronic stagnation in "decayed" or dirty conditions
.
Zeus: Sudden physical combustion, explosive anger, industrial accidents, and a hyper-focused, coercive drive that acts like a "loaded gun"
.
Kronos: Legal battles, structural downfall from high positions, and being crushed by cold bureaucratic tyranny or state-level overreach
.
Apollon: Over-expansion, commercial scatter, and intellectual over-extension due to pursuing too many avenues of learning at once
.
Admetos: Stifling immobility, feelings of hitting a "brick wall," forced endings, cold stasis, or severe physical and psychological confinement
.
Vulkanus: Brutal, forced submission to collective powers far larger than oneself, vulnerability to natural disasters, or explosive, uncontrollable crises of fate
.
Poseidon: Extreme ideological fanaticism, susceptibility to propaganda, intellectual pride, and collective delusions that lead to blindness (conceptually linked to "storms at sea" and spiritual blindness)
.

Mistake 2: Failing to Anchor with a "Real Planet": A common beginner error is trying to interpret equations made up entirely of hypothetical points (e.g., Cupido=Hades/Admetos). The rule of thumb is to only consider a midpoint equation (A=B/C) when at least one of the three factors is a "Real Planet", preferably a Personal Point up to Saturn

Mistake 3: Predicting Off a Single Transit (Ignoring the Triple Pattern): Observing a single transiting TNO hitting a natal midpoint is highly unlikely to trigger an event on its own

To accurately predict concrete events, the Hamburg School requires a Triple Pattern of Agreement: the configuration must exist in the Radix (Natal), be activated by Solar Arc Directions (the long-term developmental timer), and be triggered by an exact Transit on the day of the event—all sharing the exact same axis of symmetry on the dial
.
Mistake 4: Misinterpreting the Ascendant: Traditional astrologers often read the Ascendant as the native's personality or physical self
. In Uranian astrology, doing so completely ruins the reading
. The Midheaven (MC) represents "I" (conscious selfhood)
. The Ascendant (ASC) represents "Others" (the immediate environment, associates, and partners)
.
Mistake 5: Treating the Dial as a Personal Monopoly: Beginners see a stressful midpoint tree and panic
. In practice, not every dial configuration belongs to you
; it may describe a partner, spouse, or boss in your life
. Astrologers must cross-reference with the Traditional chart (using sign rulers, house rulers, and dignities) to verify who the dial picture is actually describing
.
Mistake 6: Trying to Interpret Everything (Lack of Focus): In standard astrology, you delineate every planet, but in Uranian/Cosmobiology midpoint work, you only interpret points that make a hard aspect or are directly involved in an active midpoint tree
. Beginners get overwhelmed trying to read all 5,000 midpoint permutations in a chart instead of focusing strictly on the active axes
