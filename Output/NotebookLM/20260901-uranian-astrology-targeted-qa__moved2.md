Thoughts
The Hamburg School of Astrology, historically coined as Uranian astrology in the United States by Richard Svehla in 1936
, was developed in Weimar-era Germany by the land surveyor and astronomer Alfred Witte
. Seeking to transition astrology from a qualitative, symbolic art into an empirical, mathematically verifiable science
, Witte introduced the concept of Transneptunian points (TNOs or TNPs)
. These points are not physical planetary bodies, but slow-moving mathematical sensitive coordinates or barycenters
that function as generational fields of energy
. They act like "adjectives" to describe, qualify, and complete "planetary pictures" of standard planets and angles in a chart
.
The first four Transneptunian points (Cupido, Hades, Zeus, and Kronos) were calculated by Witte
. The remaining four (Apollon, Admetos, Vulkanus, and Poseidon) were later posited by his collaborator Friedrich Sieggrün
.
Delineation of the 8 Transneptunian Points
1. Cupido (CU)
Orbital Period: Approximately 262 to 262.5 years
.
Core Meaning: Psychological drive for socialization, institutionalized integration, and aesthetic harmony
. Acting as a "Super-Venus"
, it governs how one binds and integrates into communal systems
.
Keywords: Unity, groups, family, marriage, partnerships, corporations, social groups, and the arts
.
Natal Interpretation: Indicates how the native experiences family dynamics, marriages, and corporate or community relationships
. Prominent placements denote strong artistic and aesthetic talents, highlighting a gift for synthesizing disparate elements into a unified, harmonious whole
.
Transit Interpretation: Triggers the establishment of structured unions
. It marks major relationship milestones, such as marriages, family reunions, the purchase of family property, or formal entry into associations
. Under challenging configurations, it can manifest as rigid family expectations or stifling structural demands
.
2. Hades (HA)
Orbital Period: Approximately 357 to 360.6 years
.
Core Meaning: A lower-octave counterpart to Pluto
representing structural decay, deep purges, and ancient records
. It represents areas where one must confront raw, unrefined material or systemic breakdown
.
Keywords: Decay, secrets, the occult, transformation, antiquity, poverty, sickness, ruins, waste, servitude, and lack
.
Natal Interpretation: Often indicates vocational indicators linked to deep, specialized work: medicine, archaeology, antique dealings, depth psychology, esoteric healing, or managing garbage and waste disposal
. It shows where the native experiences life wounds or chronic issues that demand profound inner processing
.
Transit Interpretation: Brings confrontations with past-due karmic lessons, physical or psychological purges, and periods of deep isolation
. It represents a time for clearing out debris, renovating outdated structures, and addressing old, chronic issues
. Though challenging, it offers the essential raw material for deep healing and spiritual rejuvenation
.
3. Zeus (ZE)
Orbital Period: Approximately 448 to 455.6 years
.
Core Meaning: Highly focused, targeted, and directed creative and assertive energy
. Unlike the impulsive nature of Mars, Zeus acts as a planned, calculated, and disciplined drive
.
Keywords: Directed energy, leadership, creativity, procreation, machinery, industrialization, fire, heat, firearms, and compulsive drive
.
Natal Interpretation: Points to where the native possesses an intense executive focus, mechanical aptitude, and a targeted, uncompromising approach to achieving ambitions
. It represents strong leadership capabilities and creative production
.
Transit Interpretation: Serves as a catalyst for launching technically complex or ambitious projects
. It is the spark of action and the physical force to build
. If negatively aspected by Mars or Saturn, it can trigger sudden combustion, industrial accidents, explosive arguments, or direct conflicts with authority figures
.
4. Kronos (KR)
Orbital Period: Approximately 512 to 522 years
.
Core Meaning: Archetype of the sovereign, independence, and the pinnacle of authority
. It governs official systems and represents the absolute "highest standard" or mastery of any given domain
.
Keywords: Authority, mastery, government, leaders, high status, nobility, master craftsmanship, state institutions, and legal systems
.
Natal Interpretation: Shows where the native seeks self-determination, commands natural respect, or possesses supreme expertise
. It also indicates how one interacts with high-level educational, governmental, or corporate hierarchies
.
Transit Interpretation: Elevates the native to positions of leadership, triggers interactions with state authorities, or brings official, legal recognition and honors
. Under stressful aspects, it can indicate downfall from high status, systemic overreach, or legal battles with institutional powers
.
5. Apollon (AP)
Orbital Period: Approximately 576 years
.
Core Meaning: Operating as a "Super-Jupiter"
, Apollon represents peaceful, systemic expansion, multiplicity, and far-reaching accumulation of wealth or knowledge
.
Keywords: Expansion, trade, commerce, multiplicity, higher learning, science, peaceful enterprises, widespread influence, and great distance
.
Natal Interpretation: Grants the native a balanced, optimistic, and philosophical temperament
. It indicates a strong capacity to manage complex networks of information, broad business ventures, or multi-faceted fields of study
.
Transit Interpretation: Brings times of massive commercial expansion, fame, or educational breakthroughs
. It acts to multiply whatever planetary tree it touches; for example, transiting Apollon touching the natal Sun or Jupiter triggers major material and intellectual prosperity
.
6. Admetos (AD)
Orbital Period: Approximately 614 to 624 years
.
Core Meaning: The principle of condensation, absolute focus, and structural crystallization
. Where Saturn restricts and Pluto transforms, Admetos compresses energy down to an immovable, atomic focal point (often compared to a "brick wall" demanding patience)
.
Keywords: Endurance, stability, stasis, restraint, the earth/soil, raw materials, agriculture, depth, and cyclical endings/beginnings
.
Natal Interpretation: Shows where the native possesses deep, slow, and unwavering concentration
. It can indicate deep connections to the earth, real estate, rock, minerals, and subterranean matter
. It also marks areas where the native faces profound spiritual or psychological blocks that require long, introspective effort to resolve
.
Transit Interpretation: Signals periods of absolute stasis, quiet background work, or consolidating structural foundations
. It is a time for ending obsolete life cycles and planting slow-growing seeds for the distant future
. Under negative configurations, it can generate feelings of severe physical confinement, immobility, or forced endings
.
7. Vulkanus (VU)
Orbital Period: Approximately 660 to 663 years
.
Core Meaning: The highest density of physical, mechanical, and systemic power available in a chart
. It represents forces larger than the individual—natural disasters, irresistible fate, and massive collective energy movements
.
Keywords: Raw power, vitality, mighty strength, overpowering influence, reserve energy, and monumental force
.
Natal Interpretation: Reveals where the native holds immense reserve strength, granting the capacity to overcome massive physical or psychological limitations and "handicaps"
. It shows where one can exert monumental influence
.
Transit Interpretation: Marks massive, life-altering events of absolute fate
. It pumps irresistible energy into the activated midpoint trees
. This can result in monumental physical drive, powerful career breakthroughs, or forced submission to structural powers far larger than oneself
.
8. Poseidon (PO)
Orbital Period: Approximately 740 to 745 years
.
Core Meaning: The absolute peak of spirit, intellect, and mental illumination
. Functioning as a "Super-Neptune," it strips away illusion and deceit, offering pure, spiritualized wisdom, ideas, and agapic love
.
Keywords: Illumination, spirituality, truth, intellect, ideology, wisdom, culture, and higher ideals
.
Natal Interpretation: Indicates where the native is highly open to conceptual inspiration and possesses intuitive, ethical clarity
. It denotes a soul dedicated to truth, education, and ideological mastery
.
Transit Interpretation: Triggers moments of profound mental illumination, philosophical alignments, and spiritual breakthroughs
. It is the classic timer for receiving the "right idea," aligning with a mentor, or gaining clarity that instantly dissolves long-standing psychological confusion
.
