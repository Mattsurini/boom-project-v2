# CK Psychological Extraction — Client/PAC Use of Source-Grounded Transit Work

## Summary
The web source insight that matters most is Cafe Astrology’s line that transits “stimulate what is already there.” Psychologically, this turns transit reading from fatalism into activation language: a pattern is highlighted, not imposed.

## Insight from external web material
- **Cafe Astrology:** transits highlight/trigger existing psychological make-up. This directly supports client-safe wording: “this period activates X pattern” rather than “X will happen to you.”
- **Cafe Astrology outside-in workflow:** slow planets first give developmental context; inner planets refine timing. This maps well to client guidance: long-term pressure + short-term action step.
- **Wikipedia:** broad framing of transits as predictive astrology, but not strong enough alone for counseling language.

## Insight from local DB
- **Bhava span / Gochara:** stops lazy sign-only readings and forces specificity.
- **Upachaya rule:** prevents fear language around Saturn/Mars; pressure can become growth when placed in effort/growth houses.

## PAC application
PAC themes should come from human dilemmas activated by transit:
- Saturn/10th/6th: “สิ่งที่คุณต้องจัดระบบก่อนชีวิตรักจะนิ่ง”
- Neptune/12th/8th: “อะไรคือภาพฝันที่คุณเผลอยึดไว้?”
- Venus/Moon/5th/7th: “เขารู้สึกยังไง แต่ยังไม่พูด?”
- VOC Moon: review, cleanse, draft, close loops — not aggressive launch promise.

## Communication boundary
Do not say “ดาวนี้ทำให้เกิดเหตุนี้แน่นอน.” Say: “นี่คือ pattern ที่ถูกกระตุ้น และทางเลือกที่ฉลาดคือ…”
