# CK Psychological Extraction — Retrograde as Human Experience

## Practical psychological frame
Retrograde maps well to human experiences of **review, delay, revision, second attempts, and unresolved material returning to awareness**. This is a communication/content bridge, not automatically a proven causal psychological mechanism.

## Useable client-language patterns
- “This is not failure; it is unfinished processing.”
- “The theme comes back because the decision, desire, or lesson was incomplete.”
- “Delay may expose what was skipped the first time.”
- “Return does not always mean reunion; it can mean closure, correction, or re-evaluation.”

## PAC-safe applications
- Love: old feelings, unfinished conversations, re-checking desire.
- Career: revision, rework, delayed approval, re-negotiation.
- Self: rumination risk; turn it into review with a concrete next action.

## Caveat
Psychological framing must be labeled as synthesis/content language unless backed by psychology DB or research literature. Do not pretend classical astrology proves modern psychological claims.
