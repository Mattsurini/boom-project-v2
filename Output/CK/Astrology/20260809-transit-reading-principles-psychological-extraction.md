# CK Psychological Extraction — Transit Reading as Client Guidance

## Summary
Transit work is psychologically useful when it names **pressure + choice-point + timing window**. It becomes harmful when framed as deterministic fate.

## Psychological Framing

### 1) Transit as “activation,” not fixed destiny
A transit should be delivered as an activation of a natal pattern. This helps the client understand what is being stimulated without losing agency.

### 2) Slow planets = developmental season
Saturn/Uranus/Neptune/Pluto should usually be explained as months-long restructuring, disruption, dissolution, or intensification. Exact dates matter, but they are peaks in a longer field.

### 3) Fast planets/Moon = mood and action timing
Moon/Mercury/Venus/Mars are better for practical scheduling, communication tone, emotional temperature, and PAC timing. They should not be overblown as life-changing alone.

### 4) Client-safe language
Use: “มีแรงกดให้จัดระบบ/ตัดสินใจ/เปิดบทสนทนา”
Avoid: “จะพัง/โดนทิ้ง/แน่นอน” unless there is strong repeated evidence and even then present as risk, not curse.

## Practical Application for BooM/PAC
- Transit report should end with “ทำอะไรดีวันนี้/ช่วงนี้” not only symbolic meaning.
- PAC hooks should map transit to human dilemma: closure, waiting, confession, self-worth, no-contact, decision fatigue.
- If Moon is VOC, frame as low-commitment / review / draft / cleanse; avoid launch promises unless other electional factors dominate.
