The database support for psychological claims about retrograde is weaker than the technical astrological support. The safe psychological framing is: retrograde correlates symbolically with review, reprocessing, delay, reversal, ambiguity, and returning to unfinished material — but this is **interpretive synthesis**, not directly proven by the classical excerpts.
