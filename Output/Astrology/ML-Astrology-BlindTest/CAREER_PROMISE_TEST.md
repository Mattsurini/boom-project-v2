# Career-Promise Test (proxy for "will get a job")

Since no public horary question→outcome dataset exists, tested the closest available claim with real data:
**Do people who demonstrably succeeded in careers have stronger natal career indicators than random births?**

## Design
- Group A: 47 verified professionals from Astro-Databank (29 scientists, 30 athletes subset with birth time)
- Group B: 300 simulated random births (1930–1990, global lat/lon), same scoring code
- Pre-registered **career-promise score**: L10 dignity (+2/+2) + angularity (+1.5), planets in 10th (+1 each), Sun–Jupiter/Saturn harmonious aspects (+1 each), MC-ruler conjunct MC (+1.5)

## Result
| Group | N | Mean | Median |
|---|---|---|---|
| A Successful | 47 | 2.30 | 2 |
| B Random | 300 | 2.18 | 2 |

Mann-Whitney U = 7586, z = 0.84, **one-sided p = 0.20 → not significant**

## Verdict
Successful professionals' charts do NOT show stronger classical career indicators than random births. Combined with the occupation blind test (52.3%, p=0.44): two independent tests, no signal.

Note: true horary job-question testing still requires BooM's own question→outcome log (no public dataset exists).
