## 3. LOGICAL GAPS

### G1 — THE most serious: the blueprint's core premise is contradicted by the research's own evidence, and no one resolved it.
The blueprint's Objective/Success-Markers demand a **reproducible "Relationship Quality Scorecard" that grades good vs. bad** and separates case groups at ≥80%. But the evidence the research itself produced says the opposite:
- Q6: sources "refuse to promise success or failure… outcomes depend on partner choice, life circumstances, and individual development"; "compatibility is chosen/created."
- Q7: "an astrological 'bad relationship' is simply one with high structural tension; it only collapses into a 'bad' outcome if the partners lack the psychological tools…"; the chart CANNOT tell you "success or failure," "the decision to stay," or "a final authority."
- The verified psychology (Knee, Franiuk, Wilcox & Dew) makes fit/belief/behavior the outcome-determining variables.

So the defensible conclusion is: **the chart reliably describes structural terrain + timing; it does NOT deliver a good/bad verdict.** The specialists preserved this faithfully but neither *stated the contradiction outright* — the astrology-assistant explicitly deferred synthesis ("no final synthesis… is the writer's step"), and the psychology specialist handed over the substrate without declaring the blueprint's verdict-scorecard ambition unsupported. **The writer must be directed to replace the verdict-scorecard framing with a terrain + fit-conditioned reading.** If the writer instead builds the 25/20/30/25 scorecard, the output will contradict its own evidence base.

### G2 — The weights are fabricated precision passed through as "functional weights."
Presented as if derived (Q6: "assign distinct 'functional weights'"). They are a pipeline invention. Both specialists flagged it; the writer must treat the four-component *structure* (7th/lord, DK, D9, UL — each classically grounded) as real, and the *numerics* as an untested hypothesis requiring the case-set validation that doesn't exist yet. Do not let a number survive into prose as if cited.

### G3 — "Consistent breakdown pattern — not maybe. consistently." (2nd-from-UL) is near-determinism from a single practitioner source.
The source (NotebookLM ref bea54abb = r/Nakshatras-style practitioner) asserts determinism; the underlying mechanics are classical (BPHS Ch.30 sl.7-12), but the *deterministic phrasing* is not. This is the one place the corpus overstates certainty, and both specialists flagged it. ✅ Preserve the flag in the writer brief.

### G4 — The astrology→psychology mappings are illustrative analogies, not findings. Correctly downgraded by the research-specialist:
- **Mars–Moon reactivity → Fearful-Avoidant** = [PARTIAL→SPECULATIVE]. The fearful-avoidant construct is real (Bartholomew 1990); the astrological link has zero empirical support. The leap is unfounded as evidence and acceptable only as a heuristic illustration. ✅ handled.
- **Saturn-in-7th = "cosmic superglue"/endurance** = a *frame effect*, not a validated placement effect. Classical texts say sickly spouse/harsh speech (BPHS Ch.18 sl.8-9, Sanketanidhi sl.66); the reframe comes from Western composite source (Sun-conjunct-Saturn) + practitioner voice. Verified: *frames change outcomes* (Knee); **not** verified: *Saturn grants endurance*. The meta-claim travels; the placement claim must not. ✅ handled (research-specialist P9).
- **"Toxic relationships have positive aspects"** = practitioner assertion; verified only that intensity+conflict co-occur (Wilcox & Dew; Gottman volatile couples). ✅ handled.
- **"Sovereign Us / green paint / third entity has its own destiny"** = source-voice framing; verified construct is cognitive interdependence/we-ness, *not* a fated entity. ✅ handled.

### G5 — Convergence claims that are category-leaps, not convergences:
- **Rahu-in-7th (classical "wife dies") ≈ Venus–Neptune (Western "enchanting infatuation")** are *different claims* (mortality vs. deception/enchantment) that merely share a valence. Presenting them as convergent evidence (Q8) is an unsupported graha-to-planet isomorphism. The writer may present the *contrast* as a systems-difference, not as mutual confirmation.
- **Composite ≈ UL** as "the relationship's own chart" is a *structural analogy* (both treat the bond as an entity); neither system's mechanism validates the other. Fine as analogy; not as corroboration.
