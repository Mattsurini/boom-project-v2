# Nan Critique: Simple Transit Method

## Verdict
**Needs revision before project approval.** Treat the method as layered signal triage, not a complete predictive engine.

## Safeguards
- transit must contact a verified natal target and be relevant to the question
- exactness alone is insufficient
- hard aspect is not automatically bad; soft aspect is not automatically good
- one exact date may hide retrograde repeated hits, stations, slow motion, or birth-time uncertainty
- use conditional language: may/can/is worth watching
- do not inflate generic sign transits into personal predictions

## Decision rule
Mention a transit only when it is:

1. calculated and input-verified
2. relevant to the question
3. within an explicit orb/timing policy
4. interpretable from a named source/tradition
5. stated with uncertainty and practical options

If unverified, irrelevant, or falsely precise: do not mention it as a signal.
