## 1. EXTRACTED TECHNICAL MARKERS (from NotebookLM results — verbatim claim inventory)

> Priority rule: items below are extracted as claimed by NotebookLM, then tagged **[GROUNDED]** / **[PARTIAL]** / **[NOT IN DB]** in Section 2. This section records the claims without judgment.

### A. The benefic/malefic 7th-house baseline (Q1, Q4, Q6)
- Benefics (Jupiter, Venus, Moon, Mercury) in the **7th house** = auspicious results, satisfied desires, conjugal happiness (Sanketanidhi Sloka 62, 67).
- Malefics (Sun, Mars, Saturn, Rahu, Ketu) in the 7th = inauspicious effects, desires "in the wrong direction," early death of spouse.
- 7th house in the **sign of Jupiter or aspected by Jupiter** → only one wife + genuine conjugal happiness; 7th in a **Varga of Venus or aspected by Venus** → "many wives to enjoy" (Sloka 65).
- **Lord of 7th a malefic** → spouse "speaks harshly and flares up quickly"; **benefic 7th lord** → kind nature, sweet speech (Sanketa Six, Sloka 9).
- **Moon + Saturn together in 7th** → dishonest partner with evil intentions (Sanketa Six, Sloka 9).
- **Saturn + Mars in/on 7th** → spouse with unstable mind, wind-related disease, "excessive blood" (Sloka 66).
- **Rahu in 7th without benefic aspect** → remain unmarried, or wife dies soon after marriage (Sloka 67).
- Malefic in **Nidra avastha** (sleeping posture) in 7th, associated/as pected by malefics → loss of wife; benefic association cancels (Sanketa Five).
- Benefics-on-7th = happiness / malefics-on-7th = discord also anchored in **Brihat Jataka Ch.4** (Nishika) and BPHS Ch.18 (Yuvati Bhava).
- **BPHS Ch.18 sl.16-17, 35**: distinguish the *promise of marriage* from the *promise of happiness*; a marriage-yoga-heavy chart (many wives) can still be issueless/unhappy.

### B. Classical hierarchy of indicators (Q2, Q5)
1. **7th house & 7th lord** (Parashari; BPHS/Phaladeepika) — the "job description": circumstances, functional success; strong 7th lord in *kendra*/trikona → stable marriage.
2. **Venus (Shukra)** — universal Karaka for marriage/romance; governs sensory/aesthetic/romantic quality.
3. **D-9 Navamsa** — "ultimatum"/"lived reality"/"inside version"; D1 = "trailer." A dignified D-9 (strong 7th, dignified D-9 Lagna, strong D-9 7th lord) upgrades a D-1 warning ("good late game"); a weak D-9 downgrades a D-1 promise.
4. **Darakaraka (DK)** (Jaimini) — planet with lowest degree = chart-specific spouse significator; often more accurate than 7th lord for spouse *personality*.
5. **Upapada Lagna (UL)** (Arudha of the 12th) — survival/longevity/social status of the bond; **2nd house from UL** = "survival gauge" (bends vs. breaks).

Textual prioritization table: BPHS/Parashara → 7th & 7th lord + Vimshottari timing; Jaimini Sutras → DK & UL; Phaladeepika → 7th-house planets + planet-nature timing; Sanketanidhi → 7th house & D-9 (Sloka 62, 65).

Layer conflicts: 7th lord (archetypal) vs DK (chart-specific); D1 promise vs D9 strength; 7th house (partner quality) vs UL affliction (bond survival). **7th lord = DK** = "single-channel"/potent convergence.

### C. "Bad relationship" rules (Q3, Q9)
- **7th lord in 6/8/12 (Dusthana)**: 6th → obstacles/family opposition/legal/financial stress; 8th → *Maraka* (2nd from 7th), shared crisis/transformation; 12th → loss/sacrifice/geographical separation.
- **Debilitated/combust 7th lord or Venus**: diminished expression, lack of conjugal happiness, spouse with lower intelligence or deformities (*Vilupta/Deena* state).
- **Papa Kartari**: house hemmed between malefics (Sanketanidhi Sanketa Three Sloka 1: child/bond dies if 1st & 7th hemmed between malefics).
- **Mangal (Kuja) Dosha**: Mars in 7th = fire/explosive fights/aggressive partner; Mars or Rahu in 7th unaspected by benefics → wife dies soon after marriage.
- **Cancellations**: (1) **Neecha Bhanga** (lord of debilitation sign in kendra from Moon/Lagna); (2) **Jupiter's benefic aspect** as ultimate savior (Sanketanidhi Sanketa Three Sloka 5); (3) **Yogakaraka** status — a naturally malefic planet becomes functional benefic (e.g., Mars for Cancer) and can "protect" a marriage (2nd-from-UL); (4) **Vipreet Raja Yoga** — 7th lord in dusthana while also ruling a dusthana → "double-negative" produces favorable material outcomes through struggle; (5) **KP 7th-cusp Sub-Lord (CSL)**: if CSL connects to houses **2, 7, 11**, marriage is "promised and functional" even if 7th lord is in 6/8/12.

### D. D-9 Navamsa as quality arbiter (Q5)
- Strong D-9 (7th house, D-9 Lagna, dignified D-9 7th lord) → overcomes D-1 warning; relationship "good late game."
- Weak/debilitated D-9 (e.g., Venus in Virgo) with excellent D-1 → reality fails to match promise; D-9 governs what happens *after* marriage.

### E. Upapada Lagna & 2nd-from-UL as survival indicator (Q2, Q5, Q6, Q9)
- Benefics (Jupiter, Venus, Moon, Mercury) in 2nd-from-UL → "bends but doesn't break."
- Malefics (Saturn, Mars, Rahu, Ketu, Sun) in 2nd-from-UL → tested hard; **lord also afflicted = "consistent breakdown pattern."**
- Planet flavors on/from UL: Saturn = heaviness/duty/long separations; Mars = aggression/explosive fights; Rahu = deception/unconventional pressure; Ketu = disconnection ("married on paper"); Sun = ego standoffs; **Yogakaraka = protection**.
- UL sign = broad spouse sketch; **nakshatra of the 12th lord** = behavioral/physical texture; UL in 12th = private/long-distance.

### F. Relationship Quality Scorecard weights (Q6)
- **7th house & 7th lord: ~25%** ("Job Description") — BPHS/Sanketanidhi baseline (Sloka 62, 67).
- **Darakaraka (DK): ~20%** ("Soul Personality") — Jaimini/K.N. Rao; 7L=DK strengthens weight.
- **D-9 Navamsa: ~30%** ("Ultimatum") — the inside version.
- **Upapada Lagna: ~25%** ("Survival Gauge") — 2nd-from-UL decides bends vs. breaks.
- Not literal numbers in the classics — "functional weights"; mitigated by cancellation hierarchy.
- **What the classics refuse to promise**: (1) success/failure (only structural dynamics); (2) marriage itself; (3) free-will override (destiny "ever-changing," compatibility is "chosen/created"); (4) a specific person (only pattern/type).

### G. Western/Vedic arbitration (Q8)
- Western Composite Chart (incl. Davison) = "birth chart of the relationship" / "third entity"; Vedic UL = bond as separate entity.
- Western synastry = short-term/daily; Composite = ultimate fate; Vedic D1 = trailer, D-9/UL = ultimatum.
- Convergence: relationship-as-third-entity (composite ≈ UL); **Saturn as "cosmic superglue"** (Western Sun conjunct Saturn composite = longevity; Vedic Saturn well-placed in/from UL = "rock solid"); transits as triggers not solo predictors.
- Contradiction: Vedic treats malefics-in-7th as inauspicious; Western treats squares/oppositions as "cosmic gym memberships" (growth); Venus-Neptune = enthralling infatuation vs. Vedic Rahu-in-7th = illusions/cunning/maverick.
- Dignity logic (Vedic exaltation/debilitation) vs. Western midpoint mathematics — Vedic sees Western as "too meta and abstract."

### H. Over/under-weighting challenge (Q9)
- **Saturn-in-7th**: classical = discord/delay, but modern = structures commitment, "cosmic superglue" → **over-weighted as negative**.
- **Mars-in-7th**: classical = anger/death; modern = "protective armor"; if Yogakaraka → protects → **context-dependent**.
- **7th lord in 6/8/12**: crisis/loss classically; can produce devoted/service-oriented unions → **over-weighted as "bad."**
- **D9 affliction / 2nd-from-UL**: under-weighted by beginners → **"ultimatum" markers under-weighted.**

### I. "Good/bad" boundary — psychology (Q7)
- Chart = "natal promise/terrain"; psychological response = "climate." Destined-belief collapses under bad transits; Growth-belief uses them as catalysts.
- Gottman: compatibility is created (bids for connection), not found; relationship = third entity with own destiny.
