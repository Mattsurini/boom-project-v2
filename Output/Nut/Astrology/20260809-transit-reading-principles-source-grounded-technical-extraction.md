## Technical findings

### 1) Transit is a relationship between moving planets and natal chart factors
**External web material:** Cafe Astrology states the natal chart is a snapshot carried through life; as planets/points move, they form relationships to natal planets/points. It gives Saturn square natal Sun and Saturn transiting through a natal house as examples.

**External web material:** Wikipedia states astrological transits interpret ongoing planetary movement as planets transit the horoscope, usually the birth/natal chart, with attention to changes of sign/house and aspects/angles to the natal chart.

**Technical rule:** Transit reading must include natal chart relationship: transit planet → natal planet/angle/house. A sky event alone is not personal prediction.

### 2) Houses/Bhava span are not decorative; they decide the field
**Database-backed:** `new-techniques-of-prediction.md` lines 1697-1701 says that in Gochara a planet may appear in one Bhava by Rasi chart but actually fall in an adjacent Bhava when Bhava span is scrutinized, and this is significant.

**External web material:** Cafe Astrology shows the same conceptual need in Western language: if Saturn falls in natal 3rd house, Saturn is transiting through the 3rd house.

**Technical rule:** Use the actual house/Bhava, not sign-only shorthand. Label local chart vs natal overlay separately.

### 3) Slow planets give context; inner planets refine timing
**External web material:** Cafe Astrology recommends working outside-in: outer/slower planet transits and cycles first for overview/context, then inner planet cycles/transits refine predictions. It names Uranus/Neptune/Pluto major aspects to natal Sun/Moon/Mercury/Venus/Mars/ASC/MC, Saturn transits, and Jupiter cycle.

**Database-backed:** `new-techniques-of-prediction.md` lines 4944-4953 notes difficulty fixing time of events with slow Grahas and uses progressed Moon/secondary directions as a timing aid.

**Technical rule:** Slow planet = season; trigger/timing layer = Moon, fast planets, exact hit, progressed Moon, dasha/annual chart.

### 4) Benefic/malefic must be judged by house logic
**Database-backed:** `new-techniques-of-prediction.md` lines 741-746 says malefics transiting 3/6/10/11 Upachaya can make matters flourish; not every malefic transit is negative.

**Technical rule:** Saturn/Mars in work-growth houses can be productive pressure. Do not fearmonger.

### 5) Transits stimulate existing patterns, not create fate from zero
**External web material:** Cafe Astrology explicitly says transits “act to stimulate what is already there,” highlighting/triggering parts of the psychological make-up.

**Technical rule:** Natal promise is the gate. Transit activates; it does not invent a promise absent from the chart.
