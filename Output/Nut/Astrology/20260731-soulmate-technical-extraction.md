## 1. TECHNICAL MARKERS EXTRACTED (with DB cross-reference)

### A. The 7th House (Yuvati Bhava) — the marriage/spouse core
- **7th lord in own sign or exaltation → full happiness through wife/marriage.** (BPHS Ch.18 sl.1; Jataka Alankara Ch.2 sl.23: "7th lord + 2nd lord in own signs → one wife only")
- **7th lord in 6th/8th/12th (dusthana) → sick wife / distress through wife.** (BPHS Ch.18 sl.2; Jataka Alankara Ch.2: dusthana lords → lost/repeated marriage)
- **Strong 7th lord + benefic aspect → wealthy, honourable, happy, fortunate.** (BPHS Ch.18 sl.4-5)
- **7th lord debilitated/combust/enemy's sign → sick wives, many wives, misery.** (BPHS Ch.18 sl.4-5)
- **Afflictions to 7th house/lord → evils to spouse; if bereft of strength → spouse destroyed.** (BPHS Ch.18 sl.16-17)
- **Benefics aspecting 7th → happy, loving marriage; malefics → quarrelling, miserable union.** (Brihat Jataka Ch.4 sl.1-9)
- **Mars aspecting 7th → quarrelsome wife; Sun+Saturn aspect → union full of dissension.** (Brihat Jataka Ch.4)
- **For a woman: 7th house = husband; 8th = widowhood.** (Brihat Jataka Ch.24; BPHS Ch.80)

### B. Venus (Śukra) — karaka of marriage
- **Venus = karaka of wife and marriage.** (BPHS Ch.32 Karakatwas)
- **The 7th house from Venus denotes the wife.** (BPHS Ch.32 sl.35-36)
- **Venus in Yuvati → exceedingly libidinous; Venus with malefic → loss of wife.** (BPHS Ch.18 sl.3)
- **Venus afflicted by malefics from 4th/8th, or placed between malefics → death of wife.** (Brihat Jataka Ch.23)
- **For women, Jupiter is the primary spouse karaka.** (Web — vedicreport.com; DB silent on female karaka explicitly)

### C. The Navamsa (D-9) — the "real marriage chart"
- **Navamsa = the chart of the spouse. Married life is judged from Navamsa; the 7th house of Navamsa directly concerns the spouse.** (BPHS Ch.7)
- **D-9 7th house sign/lord/occupants describe the actual quality of married life.** (Web — Jagannath Hora D9 guide)
- **D1 shows promise; D9 shows the unfoldment. When D1 and D9 contradict on marriage, D9 indications usually align more accurately with what unfolds.** (Web — Jagannath Hora)
- **Vargottama planets (same sign in D1 & D9) carry particular strength.** (Web — Jagannath Hora)

### D. Darakaraka (DK) — the Jaimini spouse significator
- **DK = the lowest-degree planet (chara karaka of spouse), 7th in the Jaimini karaka hierarchy (Atma→Amatya→Bhratri→Matri→Putra→Gnatika→Dara).** (jaimini_astrology.doc — B.K. Kumar; verified via web)
- **DK's planet/sign/house/conjunctions describe spouse character at a chart-specific layer.** (Web — Jagannath Hora)
- **DK exalted → favourable spouse circumstances; DK debilitated → complications unless cancelled.** (Web — Jagannath Hora)
- **DK in the 7th house (rashi) = strongest natural placement ("double resonance").** (Web — Jagannath Hora)
- **DK mahadasha = one of the strongest meeting/timing windows.** (Web — Jagannath Hora)
- **When 7th lord = DK (same planet), a single mahadasha activates both archetypal and chart-specific spouse channels → potent marriage timing.** (Web — Jagannath Hora)
- **DK + Upapada Lagna (UL) + 7th cusp sub-lord (KP) integration = practitioner-grade reading.** (Web — Jagannath Hora)

### E. Upapada Lagna (UL)
- **Upa Pad strong/weak → happiness/misery through the spouse.** (BPHS Ch.30, line ~4028)
- **UL = marriage circumstances and family environment.** (Web — Jagannath Hora)

### F. Marriage timing
- **BPHS Ch.18 sl.22-34: 13 age-based rules.** Venue factors: 7th lord position, Venus dignity/position, Moon, dasha-dispositor relationships. (BPHS Ch.18)
- **KN Rao / Jaimini method: Chara Dasha via the 7th house of the Navamsa and the Saptamsha (D-7); DK placed in Saptamsha with dignity → good spouse.** (Jaimini Chara Dasha in Astrology.doc — worked examples: Amitabh, Abhishek)
- **KP: 7th cusp sub-lord determines whether/when marriage fructifies.** (Web — Jagannath Hora; Horary 6th Reader)
- **Venus/Jupiter/Moon/7th-lord dashas as marriage-activating windows.** (Web — general)

### G. Guna / Ashtakoota matching **[NOT IN DB — flagged for acquisition wishlist]**
- **8 kootas, 36 gunas, computed from Moon sign + nakshatra.** Varna(1), Vashya(2), Tara/Dina(3), Yoni(4), Graha Maitri(5), Gana(6), Bhakoot(7), Nadi(8).
- **Nadi (8 pts) = heaviest; constitutional/genetic compatibility. Same Nadi = Nadi Dosha (0 pts).**
- **18/36 = traditional floor (Muhurta Chintamani). Score bands: 18-24 acceptable, 25-32 good, 33-36 excellent.**
- **BUT: classical texts treat guna count as first filter, not verdict. Longevity, 7th/8th house strength, and Navamsa must be examined first. (BV Raman: "existing practice... highly defective... horoscopes rejected simply because they do not conform to certain kutas, while longevity, widowhood etc. are completely ignored")**
- **Nadi Dosha cancellations: same Moon sign different nakshatra, or same nakshatra different padas; friendly rashi lords (Jyotirnibandha, Vasistha Samhita, Narada Samhita, Muhurta Ganapati).**
- **Bhakoot Dosha (6-8, 5-9, 2-12 moon positions): cancellations via friendly lords.**

### H. Mangal (Kuja) Dosha **[NOT IN DB — flagged for acquisition wishlist]**
- **Mars in 1st, 4th, 7th, 8th, or 12th from Lagna/Moon/Venus → Manglik dosha.**
- **Phaladeepika: assess from Lagna, Moon, AND Venus — incomplete from Lagna alone.**
- **Modern practice over-weights it; classics treat it as one factor. Cancellation rules exist (e.g., Mars exalted, Mars in own sign, or in certain houses when counted from Moon/Venus).**

3. **"Darakaraka" term: appears ONLY in Jaimini articles.** All Parashari texts (BPHS, Brihat Jataka, etc.) use 7th-house/Venus, not DK.
4. **No dedicated Vimshottari "marriage dasha" chapter in the searchable DB** — timing is split between BPHS age-rules, KN Rao's Chara Dasha method, and KP sub-lord.
5. **Classical distinction between "marriage" (social/karmic event) and "soulmate" (deep resonance) is never formalized** — the term "soulmate" is a modern overlay on the classical spouse-markers.
