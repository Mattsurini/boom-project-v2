## 1. EXTRACTED TECHNICAL MARKERS (from NotebookLM results — verbatim claim inventory)

> Priority rule: items below are extracted as claimed by NotebookLM, then tagged **[GROUNDED]** / **[PARTIAL]** / **[NOT IN DB]** in Section 2. This section does not pass judgment; it records the claims.

### A. The 8th house as Mangalya Sthana / dusthana duality
- 8th house = **Mangalya Sthana**, the seat of the marital bond's longevity and endurance (Q1, Q4, Q7, Q8).
- 8th house is also a **dusthana** (6/8/12) → can cause "delayed marriage" and "crisis and recovery" periods; not purely destructive (Q1).
- **Mars in 8th** → "affect the longevity of the partner", native/spouse prone to sudden accidents; can sour relationships with in-laws (Q1, Q8).
- 8th house governs **joint assets, shared resources**, spouse's financial standing, relations with in-laws (Q1).
- 8th house = **physical/psychological intimacy**; natural zodiac 8th = Mars governs sexual organs (Q1).
- In D9 (Navamsa) the 8th house is the "ultimate arbiter of longevity and depth of the relationship" (Q1).

### B. Darakaraka (DK) — Jaimini spouse karaka
- DK = planet holding the **lowest degree** of longitude in its sign (0–30°), the "moving significator" of spouse (Q2).
- Sanskrit *Dara* = spouse; DK = soul-level karmic contract, partner archetype, missing qualities the spouse "fills" (Q2).
- **Natal house position of DK (D1) = primary indicator of meeting context** (how/where the spouse is met); sign/planet = the *who* (Q2).
- DK-in-8th-house reading: meeting through **crisis, research environments, deep financial/occult settings**; intensely karmic/transformative (Q2, Q4).
- Spouse character with DK in 8th: private/mysterious aura, investigative, resilient in crisis, connected to deep finance/inheritance; professions: research, surgery, investigation, occult, complex finance (Q4).
- KN Rao: 7-karaka scheme (Sun–Saturn); some lineages include Rahu as 8-karaka (Q2).
- DK must be synthesized with 7th lord + Upapada Lagna; DK's **Navamsa (D9) position** analyzed for actual manifestation/longevity of relationship (Q2).

### C. Dignity / cancellation (Q5)
- **Exalted DK in 8th**: planet expresses "finest physical and character qualities" despite dusthana; Jupiter-DK → wisdom about hidden subjects, joint resources expand; Saturn-DK (in Libra) → softened, distinguished spouse.
- **Debilitated DK in 8th**: diminished/distorted expression; debilitated DK in Navamsa = soul chose "challenging dharmic environment".
- **Neecha Bhanga** → expression "considerably restored" (Q5, Q7).
- **D9 correction**: debilitated in D1 but strong/exalted in D9 → "toothless tiger" replaced by stability in second half of life (Q5).
- **8th as "Mangalya"**: a planet there is functionally tasked with sustaining the relationship; cancellation can turn crisis into long-term stability (Q5).

### D. Single-channel activation (Q5)
- **7th Lord = DK** placed in 8th = "single-channel activation": functional spouse role (Parashara) + soul-level karmic contract (Jaimini) unified in one planet.
- Result: singularly focused on 8th-house themes (crisis, research, deep finance, occult); spouse's character = direct key to marriage survival; "mirror of native's hidden soul material".

### E. D9 Navamsa as ultimate arbiter (Q3, Q5, Q8)
- Navamsa ranks as the **"ultimate arbiter"** of strength; D1-promise vs D9-reality; a strong D1 planet that collapses in D9 "will fail to deliver stable results, especially after age 30" (Q3, Q5, Q8).
- D9 Lagna Lord in 8th → marriage timing classified **"Delayed" (typically 31–33)** (Q3, Q6).
- DK in 8th of D9 → gains/wealth from spouse's family or joint ventures immediately after marriage (Q4, Q7).

### F. 2nd house from Upapada Lagna (UL) as survival indicator (Q3, Q8)
- 2nd from UL = "marriage survival indicator": benefics there → marriage "bends but doesn't break"; malefics (esp. lord also afflicted) → "consistent breakdown pattern" (Q8).

### G. 7th-of-Karakamsha (Q9)
- Jaimini indicators include the **7th house from Karakamsha** (dharmic partnerships / soulmates) and the Karakamsha Lagna itself; 8th from Karakamsha lagna = "hidden soul material" and "transformation requirements" (Q1, Q9).
- Swamsha Lagna (AK's D9 sign treated as new ascendant); 7th from both Karakamsha and Swamsha must be evaluated (Q9).

### H. Timing windows (Q6)
- **DK mahadasha/antardasha** = primary window for meeting the spouse (Jaimini).
- **Chara Dasha** via the **7th house of the Navamsa** or the sign containing the D9 Lagna lord → meeting trigger.
- **Saptamsha (D7)** refines windows for progeny/fruit of union, may overlap marriage timing in fated 8th-house bonds.
- **Vimshottari**: Venus Mahadasha (strongest romantic window; "late marriages turning into quick finalization"); Jupiter Mahadasha (traditional/family-supported unions, esp. female charts); 7th lord Mahadasha; **Rahu Mahadasha** (sudden, fated, transformative bonds — 8th-house flavor).
- **KP 7th-cusp sub-lord** = "gatekeeper" giving permission for marriage; timing narrowed when dasha/antardasha/pratyantar lords are also significators of 2/7/11.
- Priority order of systems: (1) 7th house/lord D1 promise → (2) D9 arbiter → (3) DK & UL karmic contract → (4) dasha/transit confluence (Double Transit of Jupiter & Saturn).
- **Vivah Saham**: meeting triggered when transiting Jupiter hits the degree-sum of Lagna and 7th lords (Q6 table).
- 2nd-from-UL affliction → "on-off phases" before stabilization.

### I. "Delayed marriage 31–33" (Q3, Q5, Q6, Q7)
- D9 Lagna Lord in 8th (or combined significator = 7th lord/DK in 8th) → marriage classified **"Delayed," typically ages 31–33**; delay framed as necessary maturation before an "unbreakable" bond.

### J. Rnanubandha (Q4, Q5, Q8)
- Doctrine: we marry to discharge past-life debts; **Padma Purana shloka**: *"Runanubhanda Rupena Pashu Patni Suta Aalaya Runakshaye Kshyayaanthi Tatra Parivedana."* — Every relationship is previous-birth bondage; once the debt is annihilated, the relationship ends.
- In the 8th house, the "debt" is paid through deep emotional transformation, shared resources, or navigating crises together; if "purely negative," spouses may be "reborn as bitter enemies" (Q8).

### K. Mangal Dosha (Q1, Q4, Q8)
- BPHS identifies 8th house as a key location for **Mangal Dosha (Kuja Dosha)**; Mars in 8th affects partner longevity / sudden accidents.
- Mars in 8th (D1) + malefics in 2nd-from-UL → "consistent breakdown pattern" (Q8).

### L. Soulmate vs. dharmic partner (Q9, Blind-Spot Sweep)
- Classical astrology does NOT identify a "soulmate" from DK alone; multiple markers must converge: DK + UL + 7th-from-Karakamsha (Jaimini) + 7th house/lord + Venus/Jupiter/Mars (Parashari) + D9 + Ashtakoota guna matching.
- DK silent on: legal/social construct (Parashari 7th domain), business partnerships, marriage survival (2nd-from-UL), post-marital prosperity (Venus→Jupiter counting).
- Interlocks: one partner's birth nakshatra matching the other's DK nakshatra; mirroring of Navamsa ascendants.
- Blind-spot: "soulmate" conflated with "destined partner"; Vedic *Vivaha* (duty) vs modern "soulmate" ideology not distinguished.
