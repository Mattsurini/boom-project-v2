# Nut Technical Extraction — Retrograde/Vakri

## Technical thesis
Retrograde/Vakri is best treated as a **planetary motion condition** that changes delivery pattern. It is not inherently “destroyed” or “weak”. Classical evidence supports strength/peculiarity, reversed order/timing, and special house-effect rules in some traditions.

## Technical interpretation boundaries
- Astronomy: apparent retrograde motion = apparent opposite motion from observer viewpoint.
- Jyotish: `vakra/vakri` can carry strength and special result-delivery rules; needs source-specific handling.
- KP/Prashna: retrograde significators may delay/reject immediate fruition until direct in some workflows.
- Modern Western/PAC: “review, revisit, return” is usable content language but should not be sold as deterministic without stronger sources.
