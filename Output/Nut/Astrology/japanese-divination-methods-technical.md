# Technical Extraction: The Systems of Japanese Divination (uranai 占い)

## Core term
**Uranai (占い)** = umbrella for all fortune-telling in Japan: daily horoscopes, omikuji, blood type, palms, names, cards, and East-Asian calendrical calculations. Broader than "psychic reading"; psychic sight = **reishi (霊視)**, medium = **reinōsha (霊能者)**.

## Method taxonomy (system → medium → usage level)

### A. Everyday / casual
1. **Western zodiac horoscope (seiza uranai)** — morning-TV *Today's Fortune Countdown* (Fuji TV Mezamashi) ranks all 12 signs luckiest→unluckiest with love luck, lucky color, food, avoidance tip. Magazines too. Light, disposable.
2. **Blood-type fortune (ketsuekigata uranai)** — uniquely Japanese pseudo-system. Type A=careful/responsible, B=independent/self-centered, O=relaxed/sociable, AB=mysterious. Social game, self-introductions, dating banter; known unscientific.
3. **Roku yō (六曜)** — six-day lucky/unlucky calendar (each date marked auspicious/inauspicious, e.g. taian 大安 vs. butsumetsu 仏滅), read on regular calendars to pick wedding/funeral/start dates. Hemerological, hemerology dates back to onmyōdō.

### B. Shrine/temple-based
4. **Omikuji (御神籤)** — paper fortune drawn at Shinto shrine / Buddhist temple; overall luck rank (great blessing→blessing→small blessing→future blessing→bad luck: 大吉・吉・中吉・小吉・末吉・凶) + advice on love/work/health/travel/money. ~1,000-year history from judgment-lots. Bad luck tied to rack/tree to leave behind. #1 tourist-accepted form.
5. **Koimikuji (恋御神籤)** — love-focused omikuji; direct advice (don't rush / let go / confess).
6. **Omamori (お守り)** — protective charm to carry; not a prediction, a wish.
7. **Enkiri / enmusubi** — rite/spot (e.g. Yasui Konpira-gū stone) to sever unwanted ties or form good ones; relationship-focused power spots.

### C. Systematic "Eastern" calculations (serious consultation)
8. **Shichūsuimei (四柱推命)** — Four Pillars of Destiny (BaZi, Japanese school). Reads from birth year/month/day/hour; life path, career, compatibility. Logical/statistical feel; flagship of serious salons.
9. **Kyūsei kigaku (九星気学)** — Nine Star Ki; uses birth date + nine star numbers, compass direction, year cycles; luck by year/direction. Common in Kyoto/Tokyo salons and on calendars.
10. **Onmyōdō (陰陽道)** — historical yin-yang/five-element (gogyō) + sexagenary cycle system from the court Bureau of Divination (Onmyōryō, 7th c.); astrology, calendars, directions, geomancy (fūsui), kimon "demon gate." Cultural root of much of the above.
11. **Fūsui (風水 / feng shui)** — environmental energy; lucky directions (bathroom east, kitchen SE, entry south); still consulted when moving apartments. Believed in Japan "since a long time ago."
12. **Takashima Ekidan (高島易断)** — Meiji-era I Ching (Book of Changes) divination (Takashima Kaemon, 1901); street practitioners near stations; short face-to-face readings.

### D. Personal / directly readable
13. **Tesō uranai (手相占い)** — palm reading; life/marriage/money lines; cheap, quick, street stalls/shopping centers/events; personal ("unique to you"). Lines believed to change with age.
14. **Seimei handan (姓名判断)** — name analysis by kanji stroke count; real effect on baby naming / business naming; often done free online.
15. **Tarot (タロット)** — familiar to younger women via anime/manga/games; in-person or online (Ikebukuro/Harajuku); more mystical than TV horoscope.

## How it's institutionalized
- **Morning TV + magazines**: zodiac rankings (casual).
- **Shrines/temples**: omikuji, koimikuji, omamori (ritual + recreation).
- **Street/salon**: palm, teso, Takashima ekidan near stations (~¥3,000–4,000 / 15 min); professional salons in Tokyo/Kyoto for shichūsuimei, kyūsei kigaku.
- **Online**: free seimei handan, tarot advisors, rokuyō checkers; apps.
- **Tourist-facing**: English omikuji (Sensō-ji Asakusa), Harajuku/Gion palm & tarot, power spots.

## Key mechanics notes
- Omikuji ranks run ~大吉 だいきち down to 凶 きょう (Sensō-ji famous for high bad-luck rate).
- Roku yō is pure hemerology — no personality, only day fortune.
- Shichūsuimei = the Japanese BaZi lineage; kyūsei kigaku = nine-star method distinct from BaZi (uses star numbers by birth year + compass).
- Name divination (seimei handan) depends on stroke-count school — results differ by school.
