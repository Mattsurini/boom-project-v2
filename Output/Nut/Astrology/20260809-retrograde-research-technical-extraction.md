# Nut Technical Extraction — Retrograde / Vakri

## Source-backed technical markers

### 1) Astronomical layer: apparent irregular motion
- B.V. Raman defines retrogression/acceleration as variation in planetary velocity along elliptical orbits. A planet becomes retrograde when its distance from the Sun exceeds a limit; all planets except Sun and Moon are subject to retrogression/acceleration.
- Source: `E:/Boom Project/Knowledge/Astrology-Database/Jaimini/A Manual of Hindu Astrology by BV Raman.md`, lines 1784–1818.

### 2) BPHS: Vakra is one of 8 motions and has high motional strength
- BPHS Ch.27 lists eight motions for Mars to Saturn: Vakra (retrogression), Anuvakra/direct-grade-ish OCR, Vikala/stationary, Manda, Mandatara, Sama, Chara, Atichara.
- The strength values listed are: 60, 30, 15, 30, 15, 7.5, 45, 30.
- Technical takeaway: Vakra is not automatically “weak.” In this source it receives the highest motion-strength value in the list.
- Source: `BPHS-Santhanam-Vol-1.md`, lines 6170–6178.

### 3) BPHS Vol.2: retrograde reverses dasha timing order
- Effects normally: planet in 1st Drekkana gives results at beginning of dasha, 2nd in middle, 3rd at end.
- If retrograde, order reverses: 3rd Drekkana results at beginning; 1st Drekkana results at end. Rahu/Ketu are always read in reverse order.
- Source: `BPHS-Santhanam-Vol-2.md`, lines 1698–1710.

### 4) Bhrigu/Nadi layer: previous house + occupied house, with perplexity
- Bhrigu Samhita states Vakri planets “take care of the previous house” and cause “great progress” of the occupied house, though with “some perplexity.”
- Technical takeaway: one Nadi-style rule supports reading retrograde as backward-linking, not merely delayed.
- Source: `Book. Bhrigu Samhita T.M.Rao.md`, lines 365–380.

### 5) KP/Prashna layer: retrograde can invalidate ruling planets temporarily
- KP article says if a ruling planet is retrograde or in the star/sub of a retrograde planet, reject that planet during the period of retrogression and use remaining ruling planets.
- Technical takeaway: in event timing/horary-like KP, retrograde may signal “not ready/not fruitful yet,” not simply “strong.”
- Source: `An-insight-about-Astrology-and-KP-Astrology.md`, lines 35–45.

## Technical synthesis

Retrograde has at least four separate meanings depending on system:

| Layer | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Astronomy | Apparent backward motion / variable speed; Sun & Moon do not retrograde. |
| Strength | Vakra can increase cheshta/motional strength; not automatically weak. |
| Timing | Dasha result order can reverse. |
| House logic | Nadi/Bhrigu can link planet back to previous house while strengthening occupied house with perplexity. |
| KP/event timing | Retrograde significator/ruling planet may be rejected temporarily. |
