## 1. Executive Summary

**The golden thread of this research: the chart supplies the terrain; psychology decides survival.**

Every layer of evidence — classical shloka, verified psychology, and the research's own blind-spot sweep — converges on a single, defensible statement: Vedic astrology can describe the **structural terrain** of a relationship (its friction points, its durability markers, the timing of its strains), but it cannot deliver a **verdict** on whether a relationship is "good" or "bad." The outcome is decided in the psychological climate — belief systems, repair skills, and fit — that plays out *on* that terrain.

The blueprint's ambition of a weighted scorecard that grades good-vs-bad and separates case groups at ≥80% accuracy is therefore **not supported by the evidence**. The classical texts explicitly "refuse to promise success or failure" and state that outcomes depend on partner choice, life circumstances, and individual development. The verified psychology (Knee; Franiuk; Wilcox & Dew) identifies belief and interaction process, not chart signature, as the outcome-determining variables. This report resolves the contradiction by replacing the verdict-scorecard with a **two-axis model** (structural tension × relational skill) that grades *terrain difficulty and capacity to navigate it* — not a verdict.

**What this report delivers:**
- A verified inventory of the astrological terrain markers, each tagged with its source-authority level (classical shloka-verified vs. modern synthesis vs. practitioner hypothesis).
- A verified psychological climate layer built from independently confirmed research.
- The convergence points where the two layers genuinely align — and the points where they merely share a valence (rejected as "convergences").
- An honest accounting of every blind spot: what can be claimed, what cannot, and the tests that would close the gap.
- A defensible two-axis "Good vs. Bad" model as the replacement for the scorecard.

The chart's contribution to the question "is this relationship good or bad" is **structural**: it describes the texture of the bond (harmonious vs. discordant baseline), its quality trajectory (early promise vs. lived reality), its survival architecture, and the timing of strain. It does not, and by its own classical authorities cannot, predict whether the bond will succeed or fail.

### 2.2 What the classical layer refuses to promise

The classics and their modern heirs are explicit about the boundary: the chart does **not** promise (a) success or failure — only structural dynamics; (b) the marriage event itself; (c) a final verdict that overrides free will — destiny is "ever-changing" and compatibility is "chosen/created"; (d) a specific person — only a pattern/type. This is the classical warrant for the report's central refusal: **no chart signature is a verdict.**

### 3.2 The frame-dependence principle

The single most important psychological finding for this report: **belief systems change the felt meaning of an astrological event.** The same "bad transit" (e.g., Saturn square Venus) is read by a Destiny believer as proof of incompatibility and by a Growth believer as a developmental catalyst. Verified: *frames change outcomes* (Knee; Franiuk; Gander 2024). Not verified: *any specific placement grants or denies endurance.* This is why the terrain layer can describe the terrain but the climate layer decides survival.

## 4. THE CONVERGENCE — Where the Two Layers Align

The convergence is real, but it is narrower than a naive reading suggests. It operates at the level of *structure and framing*, not at the level of *causal identity*.

### 4.1 Genuine convergences

**C1. Afflictions = high-structure terrain, not bad relationships.** A chart with Mars-in-7th, a dusthana 7th lord, or a malefic 2nd-from-UL describes a relationship with **high structural tension**: more conflict episodes, more rupture cycles, heavier timing pressure. Psychology's contribution: this only *becomes* a "bad relationship" when the partners lack the tools to navigate rupture and repair (P5). **The astrological verdict "discord-prone" converges with the psychological finding "high repair demand"** — the two layers agree on *difficulty*, not on *outcome*.

**C2. The destiny/growth frame decides what a "bad transit" is.** A Saturn transit hitting the 7th/UL axis is astrologically a stress-timing event (terrain); whether the couple reads it as "proof it wasn't meant to be" (Destiny belief → collapse) or "a strain to work through" (Growth belief → catalyst) is decided by P3. **This is the cleanest two-layer alignment in the research:** the chart times the strain; the belief system assigns its meaning.

**C3. Event ≠ quality = duration ≠ thriving.** The classical separation of marriage-event from marriage-quality (T10) and the psychological separation of *staying* from *thriving* (P9) are the same distinction in two languages. A marriage-yoga can coexist with an issueless/unhappy union (Sanketanidhi sl.72), just as a couple can stay together with high satisfaction + high conflict + higher divorce risk (Wilcox & Dew 2010). **Both layers agree: the fact of the bond and the quality of the bond are different objects.**

**C4. The honeymoon fade maps onto the D-1/D-9 distinction.** The verified trajectory finding — destiny believers start higher but decline, growth believers decline more slowly (Gander et al. 2024) — is a striking analogue to the modern D-1 "trailer" vs. D-9 "lived reality" model (T4), and to limerence's early-intensity-then-fade profile (Evans 2026). Both layers describe the same temporal arc: **initial promise is not the lived reality.** This is an analogy between an unvalidated astrological framing and a verified psychological pattern — it illustrates, it does not prove.

**C5. Relationship-as-entity is a shared structural metaphor.** Vedic UL and Western composite both treat the bond as an entity with its own profile (T6; composite doctrine — **[NOT IN DB, imported web]**). Psychology's verified construct is cognitive interdependence/we-ness (P4). **The convergence is at the level of the metaphor, not the mechanism** — neither astrological system's mechanics validate the other, and the "destiny" flavor of "Sovereign Us" is source-voice language, not a finding.

### 4.2 Rejected pseudo-convergences

- **Positive-aspect toxicity as a causal astrological finding** (P9): the practitioner assertion that "toxic relationships usually have extremely positive aspects" is *not* an astro-outcome finding. Verified only that intensity and conflict co-occur. It converges with the terrain/climate model as a **warning about the limits of good aspects**, not as a predictive rule.
- **Mars-Moon → Fearful-Avoidant as causal** (P2): rejected as a causal claim; admissible only as an *illustrative mapping* of a reactivity terrain marker onto a real attachment construct (§2.3).
- **Rahu ≈ Neptune** (Q8): rejected as a convergence; it is a systems-contrast (§2.3).
- **Composite = UL = third entity as corroboration** (Q8): rejected as corroboration; both treat the bond as an entity, but neither validates the other (§2.3).

## 5. Blind-Spot Resolution

Each blind spot from the blueprint's sweep and the audit is resolved honestly: what **can** be claimed, what **cannot**, and what test would close it.

### B1 — No verified outcome dataset (scorecard unvalidated)
- **Can claim:** the four-component *structure* (7th/lord, DK, D-9, UL) is classically grounded as the correct layers of judgment (T1-T6).
- **Cannot claim:** that any combination of them separates good from bad relationships — **no study has mapped astrological signatures onto validated outcomes** (Dyadic Adjustment Scale, divorce records, longevity) across cultures.
- **To close it:** a peer-reviewed longitudinal study linking specific chart signatures to **Spanier's Dyadic Adjustment Scale (1976, *JMF* 38(1):15-28)** scores and official divorce/separation records, across diverse cultures. This is the single highest-value open research item.

### B2 — Belief × "bad synastry" interaction untested
- **Can claim:** belief systems are verified moderators of how conflict/friction is read and engaged (Knee; Franiuk 2004; Gander 2024). It is therefore *plausible* that a Growth-belief couple with malefic placements outlasts a Destiny-belief couple with benefic placements.
- **Cannot claim:** that this interaction statistically overrides "bad synastry" — **no study crosses astrological aspect × belief system × outcome**. The astrology half is unmeasured.
- **To close it:** a study operationalizing a specific "bad" signature (e.g., Mars-square-Moon) × Knee's ITR subscales × DAS + longevity.

### B3 — Aspect × 5:1 interaction ratio untested
- **Can claim:** the 5:1 ratio and its divorce thresholds are verified (Gottman; UW News 2004).
- **Cannot claim:** that challenging aspects predict the *failure* of the 5:1 ratio — **no study correlates natal aspect configurations with observed interaction ratios** (SPAFF-coded conflict, Four Horsemen).
- **To close it:** observational coding of couple conflict (SPAFF/5:1) crossed with natal aspect signatures.

### B4 — No methodology weights the native's subjective relational needs
- **Can claim:** volatility can be a functional couple style (Gottman's "volatile" couples; Gottman & Krokoff 1989 — conflict *engagement* predicts longitudinal improvement). "Good/bad" is partly relative to the native's tolerance and needs.
- **Cannot claim:** any astrological formula for weighting natal needs. The "a relationship is only bad if it contradicts the 7th-house ruler's natal condition" proposal is an **untested interaction model**, not a result.
- **To close it:** a study adding individual-difference need-for-intensity/conflict-tolerance variables to the B2 design.

### Blueprint coverage gaps (marked open, not silently dropped)
- **36-guna/Ashtakoota matching:** entirely absent from the research (acquisition wishlist). **Open.**
- **Dasha-timing of critical junctions:** only glancingly treated (Vimshottari, "transits as triggers"). The "when does the strain land" question is **open**.
- **Partner-chart / Vedic two-chart work:** single-chart-centric; Vedic synastry un-done. **Open.**
- **Phaladeepika/Saravali verse-level benefic/malefic rules:** Phaladeepika's Mars-in-7th content is an **editor's note** (commentary); Saravali's 7th-house sl.45-51 are confirmed present (verified by audit) but were not extracted at verse level for this question. **Partially open.**
- **Provenance:** Sanketanidhi is an **1860 compilation**, not an ancient classic — its authority is tempered throughout (§2.1, T1). BPHS Ch.18 (not Ch.20) is the correct chapter (FC/E1); BPHS Ch.18 sl.16-17 = evils/loss, not "issueless yoga" (FC/E2).

### What the pipeline invented (must not travel as doctrine)
1. **Scorecard weights 25/20/30/25** — NotebookLM's synthesis with no source. Retained only as a *heuristic for weighing the four grounded layers*, explicitly labeled unvalidated (§5.2).
2. **All astrology→psychology mappings** (Mars-Moon→fearful-avoidant; Saturn-in-7th→endurance; D1/D9→trajectory; 2nd-from-UL→"consistent breakdown") — illustrative, speculative, unmeasured.
3. **"Most toxic relationships have positive aspects"** — practitioner assertion.
4. **"Guardian/Yogakaraka mathematical threshold"** in Saravali — not locatable in the local text (0 hits for Neecha/Vipreet).

### 5.2 The scorecard's proper status

The four-layer *structure* — 7th/7th-lord ("job description"), Darakaraka ("spouse personality"), D-9 ("lived reality"), Upapada Lagna ("survival gauge") — is a legitimate, classically-grounded diagnostic scaffold. The **numerics (25/20/30/25) are a heuristic only**: they have no classical source, no database source, and no case-set validation. This report therefore presents them as *a working weighting that a reader may adopt or discard*, with the explicit understanding that no accuracy claim (including the blueprint's ≥80% separation) can attach to them until the B1 case-set exists. **They are a starting heuristic, not a doctrine.**

## 6. The "Good vs. Bad" Model

### 6.1 Why a single score is not defensible

A single numeric good/bad verdict requires (a) a validated outcome dataset (B1) — none exists; (b) validated weights (G2) — none exist; (c) a resolution of the frame-dependence problem (P3) — belief systems change outcomes independently of the chart; and (d) a weighting of subjective needs (B4) — no methodology exists. Any score that ignored these would be precision passed off as knowledge. **This report refuses to produce one.**

### 6.2 The two-axis model: structural tension × relational skill

The defensible replacement is a **two-axis diagnostic** that grades the *terrain* and the *capacity to navigate it* — both of which are legible — instead of faking a verdict on the outcome, which is not.

**Axis 1 — Structural Tension (from the chart).** How hard is this relationship's terrain?
- **Low tension:** benefic 7th baseline, dignified/benefic 7th lord, clean D-9, benefic 2nd-from-UL, cancellable afflictions → terrain that favors ease.
- **High tension:** malefic 7th baseline, 7th lord in 6/8/12 or debilitated/combust, afflicted D-9, malefic 2nd-from-UL, uncancelled afflictions, Papa Kartari → terrain that demands work: more rupture cycles, more timing pressure, higher repair demand.
- Tension is legible, shloka-grounded, and cancellable (T8). It is a description of *difficulty*, never a prediction of *doom*.

**Axis 2 — Relational Skill & Fit (from behavior and psychology).** How well can this couple navigate the terrain?
- **High capacity:** Growth ("work-it-out") beliefs, a working 5:1 positive-to-negative ratio, active repair after rupture, cognitive interdependence ("we" identity), attachment security.
- **Low capacity:** Destiny/soulmate beliefs with low fit, a breakdown of the 5:1 ratio (≤1:1), failed repair attempts, contempt, avoidant collapse under strain.

**The model's two governing claims:**
1. **The chart fixes Axis 1, not Axis 2.** Astrology is competent at describing structural tension and its timing; it is not competent at measuring relational skill — and skill, not tension, is what the verified psychology says decides survival (P5, P7).
2. **Outcome = tension × capacity, with the frame deciding the sign.** A high-tension terrain + high capacity = a demanding but developable bond (growth believers' catalyst). A high-tension terrain + low capacity = the relationship that *feels* and often *is* bad. A low-tension terrain + low capacity = a bond that stays but may not thrive (P9). **A Destiny-belief frame can make even low-tension terrain collapse under a hard transit; a Growth-belief frame can carry high-tension terrain through the same transit** (P3, C2).

This model honors everything the classics authorize (they describe tension and cancellation, never success/failure), everything the psychology verifies (process and belief decide survival), and everything the evidence refuses (a numeric verdict). **It is the honest answer to "how do I know if a relationship is good or bad": read the terrain to know where it will strain and when; read the couple's skills and beliefs to know whether that terrain will be survived — and remember that a "good" relationship on paper can be a "bad" lived one, and vice versa.**

## 7. Overall Correlation Strength Statement

**The astrological layer's correlation with observable relationship outcomes is unmeasured and must be stated as such.** No verified dataset links any chart signature to DAS scores, divorce rates, or longevity; therefore no quantitative correlation coefficient can be honestly reported for "chart signature → good/bad relationship." What *can* be reported with confidence is the correlation **within** each layer and the *structural alignment* **between** the layers:

- **Astrological layer — internal:** the classical rules (benefic/malefic baseline, dignity hierarchy, D-9/UL arbitration, cancellation) are internally consistent and shloka-verified; their *predictive* correlation with real outcomes is **unvalidated** (B1).
- **Psychological layer — internal:** the climate factors are **well-validated** — belief systems predict how friction is read (Knee; Franiuk; Gander 2024); interaction ratio predicts durability (Gottman); intensity predicts insecurity, not durability (Evans 2026; Dutton & Painter 1993).
- **Cross-layer:** the convergences (C1-C5) are alignments of *description* (both layers describe difficulty, timing, and the event/quality split in compatible terms) — not mutual validation of mechanisms. The psychological findings are strong; the astrological findings are unmeasured; the *link* between them is a **well-formed hypothesis awaiting the B1-B4 studies.**

**Bottom line:** the strongest verifiable statement this research can make is that **belief systems and interaction skills — not chart signatures — are the measured correlates of relationship survival, while the chart supplies a credible, classical description of the structural terrain on which those skills must operate.** Any stronger claim would exceed the evidence.

## 8. Next Research Steps
